## 3. The items, routed

### 3.1 Two are the listing's, and the concept is their source

**The sentence that has to be decrypted** is thirty-three words with the main verb in the
middle and three clauses hung off it. The concept's own `exception` field is a fifty-word
sentence of the same shape (*every skill, class and rung the System fails to hand over —
because the person it was issued to died before it landed — routes to him as a parcel nobody
else can see, hear or sign for*), shown to the listing writer as material, and the listing
kept the shape while shortening it. The listing loop's one shape rail counts coordinators
(*and*, *then*) and this sentence has none, so nothing fired. The shelf's own blurbs run
shorter: measured on 2026-09-02, no sentence in the four blurbs on the shelf reaches this one's
length. **Fix: a second deterministic rail on the listing, a sentence-length ceiling read off
the shelf's blurbs, redrawing as the coordinator ceiling does.** A counter, never a judgment,
and its number is the market's rather than ours.

**"Its colours"** is the concept's word (*rung 3, the lowest rung Dispatch issues a livery for*)
carried into the listing without the consequence the task demands of an unmet term. In draw1b
the writer translated the same want into *licensed, proper paid, not depot paid*, which is what
the reader would follow. The listing task's rule against a term without a consequence exists
and did not hold; no counter reaches it. Counted, and the redraw under the length rail is
read for it.

### 3.2 Five are the listener stance's, and the dossier is their source

Draw 3's dossier told the writer to tell it *to a mate at the bar the same night*; read 16
found the bar's idiom, and draw 4's dossier (`carver`) moved the mate *to the other side of
the world*, which fixed the idiom (the baler explained on the page). The five items below are
what that listener costs, and the coordinator's reads counted them as the stance working:

- **The spoken opener** (*Right, nights at Aldergate. Returns.*), a man clearing his throat
  to a listener, the third draw to open on *Right*. A reader meeting the book cold has no
  listener and reads a jammed fragment.
- **The direct address** (*I want you to picture the type it was in*). The listener is in the
  dossier and never on the page, so *you* arrives from nowhere, and the reader asks who is
  being spoken to. §6 counted it as an aside; it is the frame leaking.
- **The second-person generic simile** (*the noise you make when your foot goes through a
  step*; *the way you look at a lad who's been on nights too long*; *the one you do at a
  sensor*). Draw 1's read named the *the way you do when you already know* shape and counted
  it down to one by draw 3; under the far listener it came back as *like the noise you make
  when*, and §6 counted thirteen similes as concrete. The operator's *children's book* is
  exact: it is the register of explaining a feeling to somebody who has not had it.
- **The over-explained mechanic** (*at reading distance, the way a label sits when you've got
  it up to your face, except I could move my head and it came with me*). Both anchors put a
  screen in the air in one clause. Telling it to somebody who has never seen one produces
  the qualification chain the operator reads as the machine.
- **The reading-aloud trait as scaffolding.** The dossier says the writer loves *a lad who
  reads the screen out because he cannot believe it*; the Architect writes under the same
  dossier and made it a cast trait (*Reads out loud whatever is in front of him*); the writer
  planted it in paragraph two as a licence for every status line to follow. The operator saw
  the scaffold. The trait exists because a first-person narrator needs a way to get the
  machine's text onto the page, and both anchors are close third and simply show it.

**Fix: a new dossier, and it is a new writer (a dossier is content-addressed, §146).** The
same person, telling it to nobody: as he would remember it for himself, in the plain words a
stranger anywhere would follow, one thing at a time, with no listener to explain to and no
love of reading screens out. What it keeps from `carver` is the plain-words half (read 16's
fix); what it drops is the addressee, the *straight through* that produced the chained
clauses below, and the trait. A third-person arm under the same concept is the other lever,
the one `tools/ab_redraw.py` was built to measure (`--person`), and it is the operator's to
pull, since first person is his stated position (read 4 §3, §195.2).
