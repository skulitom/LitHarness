badge, registry, bylaws, promotion inside the Guild — which is (a) the institutional-paper
premise family the operator rejected in reads 7, 8 and §116 ("again with the licenses i

level: an institution issuing the ladder rather than reading a system's. The one browsing
pass named it exactly ("paperwork as a power system"). The goblins-as-property half is fresh
and the title is good; the engine is the disqualifier. This is the operator's own recorded
direction applied as a gate, not a model preference among candidates (§84): the gate read
is the operator's sanctioned diagnostic channel, and the refusal cites his words' addresses,
not taste.

**A watch-item, not a refusal ground:** the dungeon is again "the Stair" (pilot 16 coined
"Stairs" independently) — cross-book noun convergence on the shelf, the §146 repetition
observable one level up from writers.
