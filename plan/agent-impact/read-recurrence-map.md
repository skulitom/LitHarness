# The thirteen reads, as a recurrence map: which defect families stopped, which survived the fix aimed at them, and which were never aimed at

**Status: DESCRIPTION OF A RECORD, 2026-08-30.** Commissioned by read 13
(`plan/serial-pilot-18.md` §6) as one leg of the attribution report. Its material is the
`plan/` record only: no database was opened, no prose was measured, no code was read.

## 0. What this document is, and the four things it cannot be

**Every read in it is a defect harvest and not data (§95).** The operator's own reads were
never measurement — not one of them, not the household read admitted at §148. Nothing below
promotes a research claim, admits an axis, or declares a bar. A family "stopping" is a fact
about what a person said, not about what a page contained.

**Silence is not absence, and this is the load-bearing caveat.** The operator names what he
notices. A family not named in a read has *not* been shown absent from that book — it has
been shown to be either absent, or present and unremarked, or present and crowded out by a
larger item. The matrix therefore marks **named** and **not named**, never "present" and
"absent". Where a record independently states that a family was looked for and not found on
comparable material, that cell is marked separately and there are only a handful.

**Thirteen reads are thirteen different books.** Different writer, world, seed, scene count,
listing, day, and in most cases different machinery — every one moving at once. Every pilot
record in this repository carries the same boundary (`serial-pilot-7.md` §0 is the original)
and it applies with full force here: **a family's disappearance after a fix is a co-occurrence
in the record, never a treatment effect.** The "fix aimed" column below says what was aimed
and when it shipped. It does not say the fix worked, and in several cases the record itself
says the opposite.

**Coverage varies read to read and three reads are structurally partial.** Read 5 saw covers,
title and listing and **no chapter at all** — every prose family is not-examinable there.
Read 7 **stopped inside scene 1**, so anything the chapter did later was never reached. Read 9
was recorded while it "may still be streaming", so its item list is known to be incomplete.

### 1.1 A citation hazard the attribution report must not inherit

**The fix entries and the pilot records cite read numbers that do not match the read files, and
the drift is systematic.** Four instances, all shifting read 6 down to read 5:

  are recorded at `reader-read-6.md` §4.7, under §148's ruling.
- `serial-pilot-16.md` §7 cites "reads 5/10's not-what-humans-say"; the read-6 file owns it.

The likely cause is a session counting chapter-bearing reads only, which would make read 5
(listing-only) invisible and shift everything after it. **This document uses the
`reader-read-N.md` / pilot-file numbering, which is the numbering the operator's own reads
carry.** Any attribution that keys a fix to a read number should re-derive the number from the
read file rather than from the § entry that cites it.

## 6. Three shapes in the record, stated without recommendation

**The families narrow, and the narrowing is recorded by the pipeline before this document.**
`serial-pilot-18.md` §4 states it: reads 10–12's item classes "narrowed from architecture
(system, economy, cast, iceberg — none recurred) to sentences, which is the surface prompt
clauses reach worst (§138's whole record)". The matrix bears that out — every family whose
last appearance is read 10 or earlier is architectural, and all four families alive at read 13
are sentence-level.

**Every family alive at read 13 has had a clause written against it.** E1 and F1 stand against
a plain-words prohibition present since pilot 3 plus §176; E2 against §181, which read 12
records the page defeating on the next draw; G1 against §171 and §179. **Not one of the four
survivors is a direction gap.** They are all enforcement territory, which is the routing
category read 6 §4.6 opened and whose standing question is *why the clause did not land*.

**The one family the reads stopped naming while the pipeline kept producing it is the system's
numbers**, kept off the page by a blocking gate rather than by a writer obeying a rule (§4.1).
No other family in the matrix has a gate of that kind, so no other family's silence has that
alternative explanation available to it — and none has been checked for one.

## 7. Anti-scope

No bar is declared, no axis admitted, no research claim promoted, no metric proposed. Nothing
here is measurement: it is a description of thirteen defect harvests and of the ledger entries
that cite them. No quote in this file may become prompt, directive or plan text (§97.1). No
book is compared to another and no fix is credited with an effect — the "fix aimed" and "last
named" columns are two facts placed side by side, and the record's own boundary against reading
a difference into them stands. Counts stay in their canonical homes: the decision log owns the
§ entries, each read file owns its own items, `BRIEF.md` owns the refutation ledger.
