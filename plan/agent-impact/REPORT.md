# Who is doing harm and who is doing good: the attribution report

**Commissioned by the operator at read 13** (*"debug and measure impact of all of our
internal agents… I'm not sure who is doing more harm and who is doing more good"*).
Synthesized by the coordinator from four measurement files in this directory —
[read-recurrence-map.md](read-recurrence-map.md), [gate-ledger.md](gate-ledger.md),

## The one-paragraph answer

The pipeline's **structural and mechanical layers work**: every defect family the operator
stopped naming was fixed by structure, a gate, or a mechanical rewrite, and the shelf's
entire mechanical-refusal history is 14 refusals for $7.33 with zero false positives on
the record. The pipeline's **prompt clauses on sentence-register do not work**: all four
families still alive at read 13 are clause-addressed, and across ten chapters no clause
ever moved a sentence metric. The **reviser is the one lever that moved the sentence axis**
— the largest single-step improvement in the battery, felt by the operator — and it carries
three measured defects of its own: it splits chains instead of subordinating them, it
*introduces* the manner-gloss family, and half its spend is burned on drafts a cheap
deterministic gate then refuses. The **coordinator's prose gate is the least reliable
component measured**: five consecutive gate-passes that the operator's read then failed.
And the **measurement layer is partially blind to the pipeline's own furniture**, so
several quiet-looking rows are unmeasured, not clean.

## What is unknown, stated rather than smoothed

The quiet families' silence is only as good as the reads that could have named them (the
map marks not-examinable cells). The system-numbers family looks stopped only because a
blocking gate now refuses its failure mode — no other quiet family has been checked for an
alternative explanation of its silence. The reviser numbers are one book, one writer, two
scenes against two scenes. And `--no-revise` has never been drawn, so the reviser has no
control arm yet.

## The coordinator's recommendation (the operator decides)

1. **Move sentence-register ownership from writer-prompt clauses to the reviser**, and fix
   the reviser's three measured defects: reorder the stages so deterministic gates run
   before the revision call (halves its waste); add the gloss prohibition to its
   instruction (it currently inherits none of §171/§179's register rules); give it the
   subordination move directly (its current instruction only forbids chains — nothing asks
   relations to be *stated*).
2. **Persist the pre-revision draft** (one column) so the next audit can diff instead of
   inferring.
3. **Teach the census instruments the house [STATUS] format** through the registered
   instrument channel, so cadence and system-number rows stop reading as zero.
4. **Freeze new register clauses** — the four in place stay (§127's brake; removing them
   needs its own measured case), but no new sentence-level clause ships while the reviser
   owns that surface.
5. **The coordinator's gate demotes itself on prose register**: it remains the gate for
   structure, mechanics, and the operator's enumerable items, and reports sentence-register
   as "residuals for the operator" rather than pass/fail — five misses is a measurement.

No fix in this list has been started; the operator commissioned the report to find the
issue, and the issue found is an ownership boundary, not a missing rule.
