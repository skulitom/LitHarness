# pipeline: Incumbent production pipeline in src/litharness (brief -> finished chapter), call graph, subsystem verdicts, minimal concepts

The heavy pipeline is slow because of its call graph, not its Python. The chapter-stage wall time was 1,115.5 s, and 1,105 s of that was model calls. In draw 6 (baseline progress.json + traces), the 16 chapter-stage calls split into:
- 8 per-process "Reply with the single word OK" health probes: 297 s, 27% of the stage.
- 1 arc outline that planned 24 scenes to draft 4: 310 s and 57% of the stage's output tokens.
- 4 per-scene drafts: 348 s.
- 3 per-scene summaries: 149 s.
- 3 evaluation ticks, code only, with no model calls.

Across the full 33-call pipeline:
- The Architect tool-loop seed used 49.7% of all input tokens (190,803).
- 8 of the 10 listing calls were simulated readers. §143 shows those instruments cannot see the defect: they preferred our listings over the operator's favourites 24 of 24 times.
- 11 probes used 40,863 tokens.

Subsystem verdicts:
- **Architect world graph, repair/revision/editorial, roster, conductor/store:** no measured improvement to prose. Drop them.
- **Outline:** its only measured benefit was on phi4 (§54.1). A frontier model with no outline produced no duplicates (§57).
- **Status/sheet machinery:** 38 of 269 stage-0 sections deal with it, and much of that is fixing its own defects.
- **Promise ledger:** improved bookkeeping only (§110) and leaked debt framing into prose (§255, read 20).

What has evidence:
1. The writer was over-fed world facts. In the scene-1 prompt, facts were 65% and the scene plan 11%. The page then narrates that list back (§182).
2. Our own prompt words become story content. All five failed draws of read-21 echoed our own prompt text (§262–§267).
3. How a clause is signed matters (§138). A prohibition gave 7.0 number tokens per 1k words, a permission 47.2 and no clause 29.4.
4. The provider isolation flags are load-bearing (§109, §258).
5. Long-book failures happen between chapters (§242): the book's currency was spent twice and a dead man came back.

The minimal serial needs only five things:
- the brief, kept verbatim;
- a bible of about 1–1.5k words;
- a state file of about 800 words, holding the sheet, open threads and one-line chapter summaries;
- a short arc plan;
- a chapter plan of at most 800 words.

That comes to 3 calls per chapter: plan, whole-chapter draft, state update. There are no probes, no queue and no database.
