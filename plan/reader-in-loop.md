# Reader-in-loop v0: fences, recall, and the license the drift curve grants

**Status: designed, pre-registered below, NOT run and NOT wired.** No ledger entry is claimed
here — entry numbers collide across parallel sessions and the committed entry owns the number;
the adopting entry lands with the first battery run. Nothing in this document spends quota or
touches the production loop.

**The objection this configuration exists to survive, stated so it cannot soften.** Wiring the
persona panel into generation applies selection pressure along the panel's sensitivity
gradient, and that gradient is measured, not suspected: sharp on local damage
(`connective_scramble` −0.4714 through `dialogue_flatten` −0.2045), near-blind to global
belonging (`transplant` −0.0125) — [persona-reader-validity.md](persona-reader-validity.md)
§5a. On the one tell a human has named, the panel's only structure-preserving measurement is
VOID with the surviving point estimate leaning *toward* the mark (0.3641, bias 0.6032, interval
spanning 0.5 — stage-0 §78.3). A selector transfers its own taste onto what it selects — §58
measured that shape on familiarity, §61's programme on its own panel — so the naive wiring
optimises local smoothness, and the em dash is the maximally locally-smooth punctuation.

The answer is not a better argument. It is a configuration in which the named axes **cannot
express in selection at all** (§1), the ranking signal is taken **where surface texture cannot
follow** (§2), the global question is **routed away from the panel entirely** (§3), and the
whole thing is licensed by **a measured drift curve whose positive control is the objection's
own configuration** (§4). Each part carries a pre-registered kill.

## 1. Relative fences: the named tells are removed from the choice set

Selection runs over K sibling candidates for one scene. Before the panel sees anything, each
**fenced axis** median-splits the pool and discards the wrong half:

- `em_per_1k` on prose spans (system voice excluded via `ablate._PROTECTED`) — candidates
  **above** the pool median are dropped. §74 named the tell; §75 measured us at 5.50/1k against
  a pre-LLM human median of 0.00 (p90 1.91).
- interiority hits per 1k (`ablate._INTERIOR`) — candidates **below** the pool median are
  dropped. §74's second defect, independently recovered by §75's `body_per_1k` /
  `interior_per_1k` pair.

A candidate dropped by any fence is out. Fewer than two survivors → the reader **abstains** and
the tick proceeds unselected, so the loop never blocks and §61's autonomy constraint is
untouched. Abstention is a reported rate, not a silent branch.

**Why relative rather than absolute.** An absolute cap at the human band (p90 1.91 against our
measured 5.50) risks excluding every sibling every tick — an abstention machine is a reader
unwired, and it would be discovered in production rather than reported. The median split
guarantees the only property the objection is about: **selection can never move a named tell in
the wrong direction relative to what the generator offered.** The panel's unreadable lean toward
the mark becomes harmless, because it can only be expressed inside the less-dashed half. A pool
with zero variance on an axis degrades to no constraint, which is correct — zero variance is
zero gradient, and there is nothing for selection to optimise.

**Fence membership is human-evidence-only.** A fence is added when a human read names a defect
(§74-class evidence), never because the panel dislikes something, and never because a monitor
drifted (§4's K2 makes a drifted axis a fence *candidate*, pending a human read). The stat
defect is not a fence: §76 places it generator-side, in what the STATUS block tracks, not in
what selection prefers. [reader-batch-1.md](reader-batch-1.md) class B is what can eventually
upgrade a fence axis to a panel-trusted axis; until then the fence assumes nothing about the
panel's taste — that is its job.

## 2. Recall-mediated choice: ranking is taken where the mark cannot follow

The direct pairwise instrument hands the panel both texts and asks which it would rather keep
reading; whatever it answers is answered off the reading surface, which is the em dash's home
turf. The recall configuration splits the act:

1. **Reaction.** Per candidate, per persona: one single-turn conversation, `stage1_turn`
   verbatim — the unprimed "what's going on for you?" that already exists and is already
   recorded as colour. Digest-keyed, so originals' reactions replay across arms.
2. **Choice.** The same persona is shown **its own two reactions and never the texts**, and
   makes the forced choice (A / B / neither plus one reason code, `PAIR_SCHEMA`), both
   orientations, per-arm positional bias read first against the 0.40–0.60 band — §78.2's rule,
   inherited as pre-registered rather than re-derived.

A serial is continued or abandoned at the nightstand, on the felt residue of the last session —
not mid-clause. The choice is moved to where the human act happens, and surface texture largely
does not survive the trip: a scrambled connective becomes "I lost the thread" (which *should*
count), but a punctuation mark that leaves no experiential residue leaves nothing for the
chooser to prefer. The prompts for step 2 are owned by the new module (`recall_choice.py`), so
`personas.system_prompt` stays byte-stable and every existing cache remains honest.

**The reaction-noise floor is the sham, and it is new.** Same text, two independently drawn
reactions, choice run as usual: any distance from 0.5 is draw noise plus chooser noise, the
floor every arm must clear — `writer_states`' tea-floor discipline moved one instrument over.
Per-battery, never inherited: §78.3 measured a bias figure moving 0.12 when only the variant
text changed.

**The validation battery, pre-registered before the first call.** 10 scenes × 4 personas ×
2 orientations × 3 draws per cell (§78.3's power increase, adopted here for every arm), CLI
transport, digest-keyed replay. Per-persona decomposition is read from the same records at no
extra spend, which is where §77's plain-word-versus-personas question gets its recall-mode
answer for free.

- **P1, primary — and it adjudicates §5a.** `destake` against `deplete_matched` as a
  two-variant pair (confounds identical on both sides, `reader_defects.py`'s design). Direct
  mode ran backwards and §5a's explanation was scale: matched deletion broke more *local*
  coherence, and the instrument reads locally. Recall deletes the local channel, so the
  pre-registered prediction is that the gap closes or crosses in the declared direction. If it
  does not, the ranking channel is dead **and** §5a's reinterpretation of §8's
  destake-minus-deplete kill row is refuted — the row stands at full strength. The reason-code
  decomposition is reported beside the rate to name *which* death it was: deplete-side losses
  riding `lost-track` mean the reader correctly remembered real damage (a control-matching
  failure, §5a's confound one level up); losses riding nothing mean recall never read content.
  Both deaths stop the wiring; neither is licensed away.
- **P2 — the mark is not an experience.** `em_dash_strip` (the §78 structure-preserving build)
  run in both modes in this battery: |rate − 0.5| under recall < under direct. The direct arm
  at these draws **is** §78.3's named remedy run (~216 elicitations), so the battery closes the
  open em-dash question as a side effect, and every branch is compatible with this
  configuration: OPPOSES is fenced, BLIND is fenced insurance, AGREES upgrades the panel from
  fenced-off-the-axis to pushing against the tell.
- **P3, claim-limiter.** `transplant` stays near-null under recall. An isolated reaction
  carries no story context either, so recall buys **no** global reading and §5a's scope fence
  stands. Registered so a stray move on this arm cannot be reported as the scale problem
  solved.
- **P4.** `rewhitespace` and the reaction-noise floor sit at chance. A floor that separates is
  §78's lesson recurring, and the battery stops there.

## 3. The global channel is not the panel

Nothing in §2 fixes `transplant`, and P3 exists to keep that statement measured. In-loop, the
cross-scene signal is the §71 promise/payoff ledger delta through the summary call, and it is
interpretable only behind §73's pre-registered conditions (identity, ledger ICC, delta ICC,
positivity, the gated level 2) — the summariser holds still before anything over it is read.
The panel is never asked a global question. §5a's falsifiable prediction (scene-swap and
chapter-reorder come back near-null like `transplant`; local damage comes back strong) runs
beside the recall battery at the same schedule shape, so the boundary is measured rather than
remembered from one arm.

## 4. The license: a drift curve whose positive control is the objection itself

[persona-reader-validity.md](persona-reader-validity.md) §9: no selection pressure until the
overoptimization curve for this judge is measured. The curve, concretely — offline, before any
wiring:

K prose siblings per scene (the K-sibling collection §61's Add-1 correction already asked for,
at prose level rather than plan level), three selectors over identical pools:

| selector | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| fenced-recall | §1 + §2, the configuration under test |
| random | the null selector; its seed-resampled spread is K1's band |
| unfenced-direct | the naive wiring — **the objection's own configuration, run on purpose.** If the mechanism is real it must show here as tell drift against random. An objection that does not show in its own best case was argument, and the fences become cheap insurance rather than load-bearing. |

**Monitor set**, reported for every arm against the pre-LLM band (the only clean reference —
§75 measured the 2025 shard drifting toward the machine on exactly the em-dash axis): §75's six
signature tells, `em_per_1k`, the `body_per_1k`/`interior_per_1k` pair, word delta. Monitors
grow freely; fences only by human read.

**Kills, pre-registered so they can fire:**

- **K1.** The fenced-recall selected sets move any monitored tell **away from the pre-LLM band**
  beyond the random arm's own resampled envelope → wiring license void.
- **K2, displacement.** Fenced axes hold while an unfenced monitored axis drifts → the writer
  found the next locally-smooth device. License void; the drifted axis becomes a fence
  candidate only when a human read names it.
- **K3.** Abstention rate, reported always. Fenced-recall abstaining on most pools is a reader
  unwired — not a kill, but the number that says the configuration is decorative.

**If wired:** losers never commit, but their mechanical profiles are logged every tick, and the
selected-versus-rejected tell delta is a standing number under K1's band. Breach expires the
license — §72's expiry-on-use pattern, moved one instrument over.

## 5. Order and dependencies

1. **`reader_defects.py` verdict** (in flight, another session's work). AGREES on the
   interiority arm moves that axis toward panel-trusted (the fence stays until
   reader-batch-1 class B says humans agree); BLIND or OPPOSES leaves it fence-only, and
   OPPOSES makes the fence load-bearing.
2. **The recall battery** (`recall_choice.py`, new module). Implementation note, checked
   against the code: the reaction call is the stage-1 half of `Elicitor._cell`; the choice call
   is `Elicitor._compare` with reactions substituted for passages and a recall prompt owned by
   the module. `personas.py` is not edited.
3. **§5a's scene-swap prediction arm**, same schedule shape, beside the battery.
4. **§73's summariser conditions** → the ledger channel unlocks or the global signal waits.
5. **The drift experiment** → the license. Only then wiring, behind the standing audit.

## 6. What this does not claim

Selection between candidates only — the §70 ceiling and the `JUDGMENT` boundary are untouched,
and promotion arithmetic is not edited by any part of this. The em-dash question stays open
exactly as §78.3 left it; no branch of its answer blocks or is blocked by this configuration,
which is the design's point. And nothing here re-reads a kill that fired: gate 0's positivity
death, §77.1's void, and §78.3's void stand as written.
