# The sham re-test skeleton — written before stage (c), registered only if it is needed

**Status: SKELETON, 2026-08-31 — NOT a registration.** Written after the re-pilot and before
stage (c)'s result exists, because PREREG §A.4(2) prices the modal (c) outcome already: at
the pilot's ~8.5 decided votes per sham, the probability that the guarded null floor alone
reaches the pilot-sized primary effect is roughly 0.6–0.8 — so *strong primary, sham void
under both rules* is the likeliest thing (c) prints, and §A.5 commits that an amended-only
pass "would need its own pre-registered re-test." This file is that re-test's skeleton,
committed now so the likeliest outcome arrives with its next step already written instead of
improvised over a number everyone has feelings about. Registering it (filling every slot,
sizing from data, freezing) happens only if (c) fires the both-rule sham void — and the
registration must then name the (c) numbers it has seen, per the K1a form.

## The question the re-test answers

Not "is the primary real?" — the pooled and fresh-only primary accuracies from (c) stand on
their own record. The re-test answers the narrower thing the void leaves open: **is the
panel's deviation on true pairs distinguishable from its deviation on same-book sham pairs,
under a sham estimator whose null behaviour is actually known?**

## Design commitments (to be frozen at registration, directions fixed now)

1. **The estimator replaces max-not-pooled.** Two candidates, one to be chosen at
   registration WITH its null distribution derived first and not after: (a) a pooled
   sham-deviation estimator (the mean |share − ½| across qualifying shams, bootstrap CI),
   or (b) per-sham exact binomial tests with multiplicity control (Holm) and the arm voiding
   on any rejection. The choice is made on derived properties, never on which one the (c)
   sham data would have passed — whoever registers this writes that sentence into the
   registration.
2. **The per-sham vote budget rises; the sham count does not.** A max-family statistic gets
   worse with more shams and better with more votes per sham; the re-pilot measured decided
   votes at 2–14 of 20 because "neither" is the honest sham answer. The budget target is
   set at registration from the decided-rate the (c) data measures, sized so the chosen
   estimator's resolution beats the (c) primary effect.
3. **Fresh material only.** Sham books draw from the ~1,800 confirmatory-cell pairs the
   programme has never touched; no book that appeared in any prior arm re-enters. Samples
   salted with the re-test's own stage name.
4. **The primary is not re-run.** The re-test buys shams and nothing else; the (c) primary
   it is read against is already on file, and re-running the primary "for freshness" would
   be optional stopping through the side door.
5. **Recognition discipline unchanged**: probe-before-arm, the `unprobed` class, and the
   §A guard all apply as they stand on main at registration time.

## What the re-test can and cannot conclude (fixed now)

A sham-clean re-test beside (c)'s strong primary licenses exactly the sentence "the
qualification blocked at (c) by the sham corner clears under an estimator with known null
behaviour" — the candidate-reward-model claim then rests on (c)'s primary plus this. A
sham-dirty re-test means the panel deviates on same-book windows as much as on true pairs,
and the primary's information content is in doubt regardless of its accuracy — that outcome
is written here, in advance, as a real possibility and not a failure of the re-test.

## Cost note

Sham-only, at the measured per-call price: a few dollars per sham at a doubled vote budget;
tens of dollars total. Ceiling arithmetic at registration.
