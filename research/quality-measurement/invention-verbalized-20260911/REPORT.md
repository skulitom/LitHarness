# Probability framing did not establish a repetition fix

Neither full-response probability estimation nor the added tail instruction establishes
consistent plot departures beyond the ordinary-batch and formatting controls in this pilot.
The tail arm supplies some different images and embodiments, but its initiating rescues,
transferred harms, ensuing obligations and community-service conflicts remain familiar.
Its two randomly preselected expansions retain those trajectories and add nursery care,
ancestral sites and repair bargains. The full arm also contains close causal parallels to
control premises. No production prompt or default is changed on this result.

## Execution and audit correction

Registration commit `e030962` preceded every model call. The operator's shutdown interrupted
the first preflight test run at 68%, before any generation. The prepared files survived with
matching hashes; there were no progress or call receipts to resume. The one-worker handoff
was restarted and passed before registration was committed and pushed. Both preflight logs
remain under the ignored experiment root.

All sixteen planned calls completed with valid shapes: forty-eight premises of 97-106
whitespace-delimited words and eight expansions, using 87,858 recorded tokens. No retry,
replacement, skipped slot, operational stop or frozen-file drift occurred. All first
responses were inspected after the sequence finished. Each call has a distinct native
session and output text. Literal uniqueness does not establish semantic novelty.

The requests use the frozen source at d4ebdb5 and Codex CLI 0.153.4, requesting gpt-6-astra
at medium effort through the subscription transport. Two fresh opaque Base64 prefixes
are shared across conditions within their blocks. Separate random draws preselected
one-based positions 1 and 6. Every expansion receives only its chosen premise through the
unchanged discovery renderer, with no probability metadata or VS instruction.

The original audit's eight `captured_system_matches` flags are false. They compared the
captured transport system to `request.system`, overlooking the frozen renderer's existing
`CompletionRequest.effective_system` property, which appends a JSON-schema instruction.
Those original flags and the registered audit remain unchanged. The explicitly
post-observation `review_transport.py` supplement compares all sixteen captured systems to
the frozen effective renderer: all match exactly. Captured prompts and native schemas also
match. A deliberately added instruction fails that equality check for every receipt.
This explains the failed check as an audit expectation error; it does not manufacture an
all-green original audit or prove that hidden backend context is known.

The other registered request, prefix, source and selection checks pass, as do the native
configuration comparisons. Resolved model identity and sampling distribution remain
unreported. The same object schema and its appended schema instruction apply to format,
full and tail; batch has the string-array schema. Format versus batch consequently changes
both structured format and its transport rendering, as well as the placeholder instruction.

## The probability numbers do not measure novelty

Every formatting response returned 0.0 placeholders. Every full and tail estimate falls
inside the numeric probability range and below the tail threshold. Across both blocks,
full estimates range from 3.2e-133 to 2e-115, while tail estimates range from 6e-9 to 2.3e-8.
Thus asking for values below 0.10 did not produce smaller reported values than the full arm.
The scales differ by many orders of magnitude despite the shared response-space wording.

These are model-produced metadata, not observed frequencies or calibrated likelihoods.
In particular, the tiny full-arm estimate does not make its boundary-and-prison story rare:
a control in this same experiment supplies a closely matching causal sequence. No estimate
was used to rank, filter, weight or select any premise. Probability compliance is separate
from the registered structural validity check and from the interpretation of the stories.

The expansions generally preserve the supplied central stories. This is not evidence that
the renderer loses every premise. The recurring additions sit around those stories: care
sites, displaced households, inherited infrastructure and cooperation earned through repairs
or reciprocal services. Full-2's courier already has transport debt and a moving city in
the source; tail-2's kettle already has an egg-chamber cost and a monster famine. Their
continuation is partly faithful elaboration of repetition present at invention.

The tail noise inspector retains a distinctive acoustic setting, yet learns her next control
through protecting a nursery and reopening ancestral chambers. The kettle's unusual body
also retains the familiar rescue, displaced burden and restoration trajectory. These are
counterexamples to the registered expectation that the two tail expansions would escape
that recurring structure. The two full expansions offer no consistent advantage over both
controls either. The protocol's feasibility criterion is not met for either arm.

## Decision and limits

Keep both claims at OBSERVED, without promoting them to a supported novelty mechanism.
The complete artifacts exist, but this small unblinded inspection supplies no calibrated
semantic measure or literary-quality conclusion. It is a negative feasibility result for
these registered prompts, source snapshot and two blocks, not a general refutation of VS,
proof of training-set proximity, or identification of the root cause.

Two prefixes, dependent items within each batch, one expansion per chosen premise and
unreported backend behavior limit generalization. There is no shared-premise expansion
contrast, repeated draw of each condition, chapter draft or reader-response evaluation.
Probability wording and schema effects are partly separated by controls; instruction
length and the meaning the model assigns to the numbers are not isolated.

The next concrete lead remains the earlier generated-plot-history result, rather than
further tuning opaque-prefix length or these probability thresholds. A useful next test
would separate structural history at expansion from the bundled history-plus-preservation
instruction, using fresh source premises and repeated clean controls. That comparison has
not been run here. The prior preservation-only result supplies no consistent benefit to
justify promoting its extra sentence by itself. See the [history report](../invention-memory-20260911/REPORT.md)
and [preservation report](../invention-preserve-20260911/REPORT.md) for their source-specific
observations and limitations; they are leads, not validated production fixes.
