## Representation and compatibility gate

One source-only proposal supplies a question of up to 30 words, opens_after, 1-5 basis IDs and
3-5 checkpoints. Opens_after is opening or a newly occurring event/knowledge boundary, never
already-known context. Each checkpoint is strictly later and contains only after, mode and
notice IDs: no sentence summarizing its event's significance. Modes are foreground, background,
and optional terminal release. Notice has 1-2 available particulars for foreground, otherwise
none. The same question starts in foreground, persists across unmentioned events, goes into
background and returns to foreground at least three source positions after opening. That is
a delivery requirement for this test, not a theory that all stories need this shape. No
particular concern, narrative, biography, origin event or return point is hardcoded.

Background suspends emphasis, not uncertainty; release ends attention, not necessarily answers
the question. Code carries the mode across unmentioned source units and records the timeline.
Checkpoints take effect AFTER their entire unit. Notice IDs do not relocate their original
events; earlier particulars can be remembered only consistently with source knowledge.
The question's wording stays unchanged. It may remain unresolved at chapter end.

The planner sees all source, including later events. Allowed evidence is the reviewed four
opening-known IDs plus the prefix through each activation. ID guards cannot exclude future
assumptions in the question's wording. Read the entire proposal against its opening and every
checkpoint before freezing writers. Reject material future knowledge, unsupported motive,
new canon, premature closure or impossible selective attention. A compatible prospective
question need not already have a known answer. Treat an unavailable response or structural
failure as retained non-delivery, not a prose result. No repair or second proposal; do not
spend writer calls after a failed gate. A new design would need a separate registration.

## Full readings and limits

correspondences each, optional omissions separately, plus displays and grammatical placement.
For the question's activation and every checkpoint in EACH condition, locate attention or

events or invent a rationale for actions whose motives the source did not specify?

Some interpretations are required source content. Their presence alone is not an AI tell;
record added restatement, explanatory closure, specific invention and meaningful omissions.
