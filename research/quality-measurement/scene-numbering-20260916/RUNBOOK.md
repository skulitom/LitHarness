Read BRIEF.md, EPISTEMIC_GOVERNANCE.md and beat-labels-20260915/RUNBOOK.md. The operator
approved continuing the proposed next test after the outline-role comparison. This is an
isolated authoring experiment, not qualification of a reader or a production editorial policy.

## Question and contrast

Does removing the saved concept's generated scene coordinates change the chapter coverage
the outline plans and the opening chapter actually enacts? The prior two known cases locate
delay but do not establish a cause. These same cases, with one draw per version, cannot
separate an intervention effect from sampling variation or establish unseen transfer.

Both A and B use production revision 7552d36. The six generic outline role labels are already
absent in both. A's outline request must equal the preceding B preflight request exactly.
B changes only three predeclared textual spans in book_concept.first_arc.middle/closes and
the four book_concept.debts due_scene values, which become null. The profile remains v6.

The text edits remove generated scene numbers with the grammar needed to leave readable
sentences. They retain the events, causal order and opening/following-chapter language.
The six exact before/after spans and original request hashes are in the local frozen recipe,
runs/scene-numbering-recipe-20260916.json. They are fixed before live dispatch. Preparation
requires every old span exactly once and verifies that every other request field is identical.
The two forms of scene numbering are removed together; this run cannot attribute any
difference to the prose references separately from the debt coordinates or minimal rewording.

The author's original brief, author locks, accepted events, world rules, disclosure limits,
generated debt subjects and owed developments, experience brief, chapter map, prose budget,
outline schema and all instructions remain unchanged. Author wording is never stripped.
The saved concepts and initial databases remain byte-identical between arms. No generic
text-stripping feature is added to production.

## Run and stop rules

Generate four fresh six-chapter outlines and one chapter per version, with one scene per
chapter and 1,400 target words. Use halloran, third person, the existing signed-in native
gpt-6-astra / medium provider, and the same ephemeral tool-free transport. Disable memory,
user/project instructions and search as in the frozen recorder. No provider fallback, credit
reset or purchase. Only chapter1 and drain1 are allowed. Draft order is A2, B2, B4, A4;
drain order is B2, A2, A4, B4. Run one call at a time and drain only queued work.

Limits: 40 calls including health probes, 600,000 recorded Usage.total tokens, 10 calls per
book, 3,600 seconds after dispatch and 20 ticks per phase. An admitted call can cross a
ceiling; record actual usage. No world growth, invention, publication or second chapter.
Retain first outputs and ordinary policy retries. No manual repair, replacement story or redraw.

A provider failure, unknown usage, frozen-input mismatch or global ceiling stops further
calls. An unexpected renderer input refuses the affected step before outline dispatch; the
scheduler retains and stops that book. Terminal jobs, operational exits, no_work before the
target and phase ceilings stop a book. Do not silently resume an interruption.
The inherited finalizer enforces one accepted chapter and an empty queue, without adding
work to satisfy the original scheduler's obsolete two-chapter completion status.

Acquire runs/box.lock exclusively after checking for other sustained LitHarness jobs. Hold
it through checks and generation, run no sustained checks beside generation, and release
only the holder owned by this task. Offline checks never set LITHARNESS_LIVE_PROVIDERS.

draft, including retries and raw differences from accepted text. Use stored chapter views,
job-bound plans and frozen writer requests to locate activity, use, consequence, personal
response and next choice. Distinguish planned later completion from enacted opening content.
Include omissions, contrary cases and compressed or lost responses in either arm. Do not
demand a complete reward cycle in every chapter or force future story commitments to close.

Report all request/transport/source/retention controls, generated volume, actual usage,
failures and one-chapter limits. Audit checks all live outline requests against the registered
transformation, including retries. Preserve the exact source and transformed requests so
scope can be reviewed independently. No candidate ranking, selection, enjoyment score,
human-panel label, quality threshold or popularity claim is registered. Located editorial
readings are not validated reader measurements. No new reading enters generation. The claim
can become OBSERVED only; these results do not automatically change production behavior.

Keep generated prose, recipe, requests, reports and stores under ignored runs roots. Commit
identifiers, hashes, numeric records, controls and methodological conclusions.
