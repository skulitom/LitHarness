My reading is that the complete opening and full aftermath give these chapters a
clearer movement and a more consequential setback. The most important remaining
weakness is how often necessary causal information becomes a fully staged procedure
followed by an explanation of what it means. The shifted failure also shows that
writer execution can redistribute planned events; the subsequent chapter handled
that particular shift without repetition, but one example cannot establish reliable
plan reconciliation. Neither observation warrants an automatic editorial rule.

## Numerical failure and next scope

Declared system definitions remain identical after removing only manifestation
text. That control does not establish that later holdings use the right units.
The first status line prints physical allowances: Length 10 cm, Duration 60 s and
Load 100 N. Accepted snapshots retain those numbers under ability keys. The typed
sheet first becomes available at Chapter 3, carrying Length 10, Duration 60 and
Load 200 as magnitudes, along with Inspection 1 and Point 0. At Chapter 6 it carries
Duration 120 and Load 200. These are not the investment counts shown by the earlier
trial's typed sheet.

The unchanged price model charges one Point per magnitude increase. Consequently,
the recorded Chapter 5-to-6 transition sees a Duration increase of 60, a grant of
one Point and a stock change from zero to zero. The registered stock check reports
`balanced_under_seed_model: false`: that transition would cost 60 Points under the
stored definitions, while the prose shows one purchase. The raw result must not be
normalized into an apparent pass by interpreting 60 seconds as one upgrade after
the fact. The missing early typed sheets also prevent the reused transition reader
from checking the first purchase. Mana is absent from accepted snapshots; the five
casts and final Mana 15 remain on-page observations.

The descriptive book audit returns exit 1 at every checkpoint. At the end it has
five printed column sets, one unresolved comparison and zero mismatches among
resolvable comparisons. Its agreement with the same recorded allowance values
does not repair the stock inconsistency. Nor does the successful world check.

Before another live continuation, the next implementation priority is to separate
physical allowances from investment magnitudes at status extraction, binding and
sheet projection, with a regression for this exact one-Point purchase. The stored
book remains unchanged as a failing example. This is a mechanical model mismatch,
