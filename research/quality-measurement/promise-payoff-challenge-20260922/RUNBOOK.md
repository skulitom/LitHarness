# Promise/payoff evidence challenge

Registered follow-up to the operator's 2026-09-22 request to take the next steps. This is a
bounded construct-validity diagnostic on the three existing withdrawal cases. It is not a
reader accuracy experiment, a quality score or permission to admit their semantic labels.
Read BRIEF.md, EPISTEMIC_GOVERNANCE.md and the parent RUNBOOK before execution.

## Question and evidence boundary

Can a quotation extractor surface concrete alternative fulfilments or downstream dependencies
that the paragraph-withdrawal constructor has not controlled? Finding a uniquely located
quotation establishes its presence; the proposed connection remains a model hypothesis.
Finding nothing establishes neither absence nor harmlessness. Agreement across conditions or
models would not make an independent semantic answer key. No result can open admission.

The 2026-09-19 constructor's `eligible_for_model_run=false` remains intact. This separate arm
examines that material's unresolved validity; it never scores a reader against the withheld
quotation as if it were a known lost payoff. No production mechanism, manuscript or plan changes.

## Fixed substrate and tasks

- Use all three constructed cases and the exact source snapshot and private keys, verified
  against their original hashes. The inspected book is development material, never holdout.
- Expand each case to the complete 24-scene frozen book, including scenes before the opening
  and after its registered payment. Check and record all source bytes; no retrieval, truncation
  or selection based on a model's answers. This explicitly remedies the earlier constructor's
  ending its search window at the registered payment scene.
- For each opening, ask for up to four evidence chains of enacted fulfilment in each of four
  independently presented contexts: original, payment paragraph removed, matched paragraph
  removed, and whitespace reflow. The model receives the opening and full manuscript only;
  it never receives condition names, ledger descriptions, recorded payment, sibling texts,
  expected labels or the earlier private keys. Twelve calls.
- For each of the six proposed paragraph deletions, separately ask for later dependent
  statements in the full original manuscript. The proposed paragraph is explicit in this task;
  target/control status and promise ledger remain hidden. Six calls. These responses cannot
  contaminate the independent fulfilment requests.
- Eight small project-authored checks precede the book: direct fulfilment, an alternative
  restatement, no event, explicit negation, wrong actors, future intention, explicit dependency,
  and unrelated later text. The four fulfilment traps and unrelated dependency expect no
  candidates. The three positives require every returned quote to lie inside the supplied
  decisive sentence. These are an exact, deliberately limited retrieval screen, not natural
  prose calibration. Continue the fixed book schedule even if the screen fails, reporting the
  failure prominently; it cannot be hidden by discarding cases or trying another model.
- Preserve chains of up to three exact quotes each. Count a chain as located only if every
  quote occurs exactly once after its opening/deleted paragraph. Missing, duplicate and
  earlier quotations remain recorded failures. A located chain is not a validated entailment.

## Frozen execution and limits

Use the existing tool-free Claude CLI provider, `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, native structured
output, fresh ephemeral contexts, safe mode, no tools/MCP, disabled memory and excluded
CLAUDE.md. The original book was generated by the Codex provider; this is a different provider
family, which does not by itself establish evaluator independence or validity. One isolation
marker probe precedes the eight checks. Freeze executable, code, prompts/schema, requests and
input hashes in registration.json and commit before the first live call.

At most 27 sequential calls including the probe, 1,600,000 reported total tokens, $8 equivalent
API usage and 3600 seconds. These are admission ceilings checked before each call; an admitted
call may cross a total ceiling, after which there is no further dispatch. Each call also uses
the CLI's $1 budget flag and a 300-second transport timeout. Structured fields have fixed length
caps. The provider does not promise enforcement of CompletionRequest's advisory output-token
field. Requests above 400,000 characters are refused, never cropped; this is an input-byte
construction limit, not a tokenizer or long-context capability claim. Record actual usage.

Stop on transport/schema failure, failed isolation, unknown usage, model substitution, changed
frozen artifacts or resource ceiling. Preserve every response/error; no retry or implicit
resume, replacement model, reset, purchase, GPU work or production dispatch. Use existing CLI
authentication. Take runs/box.lock and inspect competing processes before sustained work;
no tests during collection. Release only this task's lock when finished.
