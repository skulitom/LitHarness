## Fidelity and grammar remain separate from permission

All four literal checks match the ten registered occurrences in order. All four nevertheless
isolate the comma-ending award before another sentence: full-1 P42, focused-1 P37,
focused-2 P40 and full-2 P44. Exact literal fidelity does not certify grammatical integration.
The shared clarification is delivered: all four print both strike awards after both blows.
That cannot be attributed to selective narration, since both conditions received it.

The university setting is not specified in either selective draft or in full-2. Their
first-year cohorts do not uniquely establish a university. This is a missing part of a
required unit, also present in a control. Focused-1 P4 adds different questions after the
paper change, whereas the original replaces the questions with countdown text; its later
countdown survives. The compound source map does not expressly forbid other questions, so
this exposes a limitation of the map as well as an unsupported addition relative to the original.

Focused-2 P4 does not clearly establish simultaneous change on every paper. Full-2 P4/P5
places stopped pens before stating the paper change without a retrospective cue. These are
scope/order ambiguities, not proof of sequential changes or reversed events. Focused-1 P40
adds a failed bar press and moving the girl back before taking the chair leg. The other
three do not explicitly add that test; full-1's description of a non-releasing bar alone
does not establish a performed press. Variation is retained rather than assigned an efficacy label.

The whole-page gesture, continuous mild resistance, rescue communication failures, newly
noticed removal-rule omission, glove appearance, unchosen coat, unusual future-activity recall
price, four survivors and final status remain located in all four. That audit is limited,
not a complete semantic-equivalence certificate. All 42 units per output, their locations and
partial/implied qualifications are preserved locally. No hidden belief's persistence or
reader inference can be proved from mere absence of a contradiction.
