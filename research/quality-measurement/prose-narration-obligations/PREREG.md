## Fixed comparison

Only the required_narration array changes: all 42 units in the control, 31 in the treatment.

word target, compression quota, new tell ban or prescribed opening. Both use high reasoning.

Their explicit conveyance is relaxed. It is also different from hiding reference prose or
future facts: all current and future source units are identical in both conditions. A changed
obligation list has different length; this test does not isolate that length from its meaning.
