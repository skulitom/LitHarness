# Progressively constrain construction of the supernatural Chapter 1

Registered 2026-09-07 before generation following the operator's acceptance of three nested
conditions. The preceding conflict test fixed the rescue choreography; earlier compact/free
scenes retained a narrow problem and endpoint. This experiment permits alternate chapters in
the least constrained arm. It is a diagnostic, not an original-chapter replacement or a
production narrative. No quality mechanism, evaluator, ranking or candidate selection is added.

## Frozen comparison

All writers receive the same ten common units: examination setting, starting supernatural
incident, protagonist's immediate rescue goal, assessment terms, figure capability, locked
exit, closure behavior and available purchases. These are a locally reviewed abstraction of
the original map, not a complete specification of the world's unknown mechanics. The source
keeps observations/capabilities distinct from unestablished timing, eligibility and selection
rules. Writer knowledge does not automatically become character knowledge or required exposition.

1. Premise: common source and display wording only. Choose actions, discoveries and endpoint.
2. Ending: add four end-condition units and two ending displays, requiring closure, the banked
   total, broken exit, four survivors, selected purchases and final perception/status. Choose
   the intermediate course of events.
3. Sequence: retain both earlier layers unchanged, then add the original 42 source units with
   their event order, knowledge timing and scene division. All listed units must be conveyed.

The common brief makes closure/purchases available but does not force them to occur. The
premise writer receives neither the exact total nor survivor count, ranks, rescue route or
required purchase selection. The ending writer receives no victim identities, prescribed
rescue mechanics or timing estimates from the detailed layer. The original prose remains
audit-only and never reaches generation. No planning pass, critic, sibling output, literary
example, anti-tell checklist or previous defect reading reaches any writer.

Shared system: close third person, past tense, opening chapter, 1500-1800 word aim, with
immediate dialogue/actions permitted within world truth. This common length instruction is
new against historical controls; actual lengths and any padding/compression will be recorded.
No repair/redraw for out-of-range length. The detailed condition includes no fear/conflict
assignment from the last experiment. Display templates preserve message words while allowing
punctuation to suit placement; the old comma-ending ONE is not an obligatory independent span.
This shared pre-call change addresses a formatting trap, not a claimed prose improvement.

## What this can and cannot distinguish

The additions change supplied information, required narrative coverage, permitted actions and
prompt size together. This is a nested construction diagnostic, not an isolated causal estimate
for constraint count. The least constrained chapter may omit the harder rules, stop early or
create an easier incident. Audit those differences; do not credit lower burden as preserved-
information improvement. A sequence draft may compress because more events must fit the same
length aim. Comparable word targets do not establish comparable dramatic or information load.

If the explanatory habits remain in premise drafts, that weakens the specific explanation
that a detailed original sequence is necessary to produce those habits; it does not prove a
model ceiling. If they appear only after added layers, locate the changed material and actions
before proposing a narrower follow-up. No aggregate quality conclusion, effect size or best
candidate follows from two draws per condition. All observations remain unqualified.

Planning research such as [DOC](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.190/) addresses long-range
plot coherence through detailed outlines and control (abstract, Yang et al., ACL 2023). That
motivates retaining a detailed comparison, not presuming plans are intrinsically bad. Its
reported evaluation is not this project's quality signal or evidence of a sentence-level fix.
No paper, corpus text, digest or benchmark example is passed to these writers.

logical folder uses one full-1 transport slot; the folder identifies the actual condition.
The whole-run six-call limit and 110000 input + output + separately reported reasoning token

in-flight call may cross the threshold; no following call then dispatches.

Use the existing isolated, tool-disabled, user-config-ignoring ChatGPT transport. Verify
subscription authentication without generation. No direct API, provider fallback, reset,
purchase, probe, retry or redraw. A failed or interrupted request stops dispatch; retain it.
Own runs/box.lock, inspect processes before/after acquisition, and separate sustained checks

runner and imported transports, CLI entry, all systems and requests before dispatch. Retain
actual argv, raw responses, usage, all output text and failures with hashes.

## Reading and reporting

Read all six complete chapters. Audit the ten common units, the four end conditions where
required, and all 42 original units as a correspondence map in every condition. Distinguish
binding violations from allowed alternate events, omissions, changed stopping points and
unstated-but-compatible world rules. Locate how the writer learns private information, what
causes actions, whether a demonstrated motive or rule is explained again, and whether prose
includes bookkeeping, enumerated background, ornamental comparisons or closing summaries.
Inspect the opening, middle and ending separately. These are defect-harvest questions, not
an automatic metric or a ban on all interpretation, repetition or interiority.

Keep every draft in a comparison, plus exact inputs, source audits, descriptive word/paragraph
counts and full readings. Log deviations and failed manipulations as well as differences.
Update the experiment history, derived execution artifact and claim state to OBSERVED only.
Run final handoff and push only this session's source-free files. No production change follows.
