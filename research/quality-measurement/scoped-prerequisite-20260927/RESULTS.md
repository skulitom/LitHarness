# Scoped prerequisite construction

The constructor can now prove a contradiction and a consistent matched edit **within an
explicitly defined synthetic language**. It cannot certify semantic damage in natural prose.
This is a call-free implementation result, not a reader experiment or a quality finding.

The [derived report](results.json) records the executed inputs, source hashes, packet hashes
and outcomes. The [runbook](RUNBOOK.md) defines the language and reconstruction command;
[`prerequisite_certificate.py`](../prerequisite_certificate.py) owns its semantics.

## What the certificate establishes

A hatch can be open only while its designated lamp is lit. Each observation states both its
trial and its instant. From an initially consistent source, the constructor makes two identical
`shut` to `open` edits: one contradicts a dark-lamp observation, while the other's prerequisite
is explicitly satisfied. The rule constrains the ongoing open state, not merely the opening
event. No persistence, reverse implication or model-authored annotation supplies a premise.

All constructed contrasts have:

- a minimal three-clause contradiction, with all three clauses present in the public text;
- a consistent original and control, checked from their complete text;
- equal character, token, sentence, punctuation, whitespace, uppercase and line counts;
- exactly equal token multisets and an equal edit-position decile;
- source-derived labels, opaque public IDs, and private proof spans and sibling links.

The two renderers cross hatch/lamp links, rule order and observation order. These variations
are **one controlled family**, not independent stories. Other-trial rules deliberately pad the
layout so both edits occupy the same decile. Equal measured features do not prove freedom from
every surface shortcut or establish literary harmlessness of the control.

The [regression suite](../../../tests/test_prerequisite_certificate.py) checks the solver against
exhaustive Boolean assignments for every partial observation pattern in the active four-atom
system. It also checks that deleting a premise or moving the dark observation to another trial
or instant removes the contradiction; a lit lamp never forces a hatch to open. Unsupported
sentences and event-only rules are refused. Forged variants, spans or proofs cannot issue a
certificate. The constructor and packet writer recompute the proof from the source.

## What remains unresolved

The frozen development book was read through a consistent backup using the shared scene
loader. The original database's bytes remained unchanged. Every reached scene was refused by
the whole-source grammar. Source identity, revision, scene hashes and refusal offsets are in
the derived report; full text stays in ignored local files.

That refusal is the expected boundary of a closed language. It is **not** evidence that the
book lacks causal relations, contains contradictions or fails a quality bar. The parser does
not search narrative passages or resolve their pronouns, modality, event identity or temporal
scope. Loosening its grammar or accepting a model's relation annotation would not independently
justify those interpretations.

`logic_certified` applies only to the synthetic cases. `ecological_admission`,
`eligible_for_model_run` and `production_authority` remain false. No provider was called and no
production prompt, policy or gate changed. The next unresolved task is a defensible grounding
contract for natural text; this result supplies a formal control for that work, not its answer.
