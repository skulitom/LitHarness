## Scope and next lead

The narrow meaning-delivery condition is met in both treatment draws. The matched controls
also communicate that meaning, and the explanatory passages and new defects prevent calling
the package a reliable cure. Two draws per condition on one source cannot estimate transfer,
separate semantic precision from wording/salience, or explain the model's internal cause.
The original first paragraph and first scene were deliberately fixed, so their voice defects
are untouched. No candidate is ranked, selected, revised or accepted.

A narrower next conjecture concerns the decision the narration must justify: the plan fixes
the purchase and endpoint but leaves its local rationale to rendition. Control-2 shows a
prospective rationale can arise; the treatment does not reliably supply one. A future test
could distinguish having precise facts from having a source-supported reason and meaningful
alternative before the choice. That requires a new registration and safeguards against
inventing motives; it is not a demonstrated cause or a production recommendation.
