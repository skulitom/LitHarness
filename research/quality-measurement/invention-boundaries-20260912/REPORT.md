# Planning-provider differences survive identical mechanics inputs

The four chapter conditions all retain Nico's defining evidence-and-sale pursuit. This case
does not show the current plan replacing that pursuit with unrelated service work. The crossed
provider experiment is more informative: the same mechanics produce different stories under
the two expanders, while familiar patterns persist within each provider's outputs.

Astra expands all four supplied disciplines into a return-home pursuit mediated by repair,
route information and access. Three leads are Mara; the fourth is Lena. Opus produces different
immediate pursuits and mysteries, but every lead is a technical professional with exactly
eleven years of experience, and infrastructure, guilds and material problem-solving recur.
Neither provider is demonstrated to solve repetition or produce better books. Both stages
remain **OBSERVED**, as registered. No production default, selector or accepted book changed.

The plan-bearing chapters closely follow the plan's heating/log sequence. The premise-only
and padded chapters invent other staff tasks. All connect those tasks to evidence, escape,
capability or assistance in the existing investigation; none abandons the sale conflict for
an unrelated continuing repair tour. This supplies no positive evidence for the proposed
destructive planning bottleneck on this case. It does not show that plans can never flatten
other premises, nor that all four treatments are equally effective fiction.

There are meaningful differences besides task choice. The plan-bearing chapters make record
registration a separate choice to protect evidence, and that choice causes loss of access.
The other two attach the alert directly to reconstruction. All preserve the source's useful
image and adverse buyer notification, but agency and the timing of the cost differ. The
combined chapter plants the valve image early (P10), then marks an uncertain recollection
rather than silently making it reliable (P87-97). The premise-only chapter instead advances
the skill through a physical rescue unrelated to photo practice, which the broad staff-quest
premise permits. These are located differences, not automatic quality verdicts.

The late signing and larger evidence collection are still pending in every opening. Missing
later coverage is not contradiction. Chapter-pad changes the hidden film's immediate location
by stealing its containing bag, but retains reconstruction as the response to lost equipment.
That compatible elaboration must not be relabelled premise failure simply because the other
chapters leave the cartridge with Nico.

The padding placebo **did not match token load**. Additional reported input over premise-only
was 874 tokens for padding and 1233 for premise plus plan: a 29.1% difference relative to the
latter, outside the registered ten-percent tolerance. Equal user-message character counts
therefore did not yield equal token counts. Context-length effects remain unresolved. Even a
matched instance would only test this particular labelled placebo, not establish neutrality.

## What the contrast does and does not establish

The same parent mechanics do not determine the resulting story. Changing the expander changes
the protagonist, immediate pursuit, world explanation and continuing mystery in every retained
pair. In this small set, Astra repeatedly adds return-home infrastructure plots even around
Opus's different replay mechanics. That locates a candidate downstream influence; it does not
identify a training cause or prove that the common discovery task is innocent.

Opus is not a clean escape. All four plans assign their different technical professionals
eleven years of experience (`opening` P2 for Wren; P1 for Nadia, Juno and Nell).
Repairs, technical competence, examinations,
guild power, debt and concealed infrastructure recur. Its plans are also substantially longer
than Astra's, and the native transport and internal turn counts differ. This is a provider-
workflow contrast, not a length-controlled experiment or model ranking. The rule failures
above further prevent equating richer apparent detail with reliable execution.

The next useful investigation is downstream transfer: preassign a complete pair from the same
mechanics parent and have a common chapter writer dramatize both plans, retaining both first
outputs. That would test whether the changed pursuit survives drafting or returns to the old
pattern. It needs its own registration; no cross-grid chapter or extra model call was made here.
These results do not justify another opaque-seed tweak or an automatic provider-default switch.
