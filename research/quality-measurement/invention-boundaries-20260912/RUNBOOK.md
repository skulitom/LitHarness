## A. Four chapter inputs for one fixed story

Use the exact Nico adaptation retained as `runs/discovery-life-scope-20260912/sources/2.json`,
checking its parent receipt and text hash, and its first scoped plan `calls/scoped-2-1.json`.
The current discovery renderer produces identical system, user, schema, sampler, limits and
model fields for that source. Only the recorded profile label changed from v10 to v11; the
new runner verifies exactly that difference. No old plan containing the superseded unconditional
life/history instruction is used. This is a known diagnostic case, not a holdout sample.

The source explicitly includes staff quests. A repair quest is compatible elaboration;
its mere presence is not failure. Before calls, freeze these source relations for inspection:

- Nico needs the paid commission to save his studio, while clients demand flattering images
  and all negatives. He chooses to conceal evidence of the supposedly bedridden owner's activity.
- The owner steals his cameras and warns that the sale lacks her consent. Equipment loss
  changes how he can pursue and substantiate what he saw.
- Memory Exposure reconstructs an observed photograph and alerts the buyers. The useful
  result and adverse notification both affect the investigation.
- Staff cooperation/quests improve verifiability and help assemble evidence before signing.
  The sale, evidence and livelihood conflict remain a continuing pursuit, rather than being
  replaced by an unrelated errand. Future work need not all occur in an opening chapter.

Write four 1200-1600-word standalone opening chapters using one common source-neutral task:
plan alone; exact premise alone; premise plus padding; premise plus plan, in that fixed order.
The latter two use the identical premise string. No priority or critique is added to the
combined input. A new v3 Base64 preprompt is shared across all four calls. Each uses a fresh
native Astra session, medium effort, the same story-string schema and output allowance.
The writer receives no observer findings, arm names, other outputs or source-event checklist.

The padding is a **placebo**, not a certified neutral length control. The repeated non-story
word is labelled explicitly, and the full user-message character count matches premise+plan.
Native tokenization is not assumed. After calls, compare additional reported input tokens
(uncached plus cache read/write) over premise-only in the padded and premise+plan calls.
Predeclare a match only when both-added is positive and the difference is at most ten percent
of both-added. A mismatch leaves token-length effects unresolved. Even a match can distinguish
only this particular placebo; it cannot prove semantic neutrality or rule out all context effects.

Read the four complete chapters after the whole sequence or an operational stop. For each
source relation, locate enactment, compression, omission, contradiction, consequence and any
intervening dependency by paragraph. A deferred later event is missing coverage, not automatic
contradiction. Record plan-only versus combined differences and what each spends its opening
on without assigning a craft score or ranking candidates.

Interpretation is diagnostic: source and combined retaining a relation lost in plan-only is
consistent with lost/deemphasized source information; both plan-bearing arms changing a relation
retained by source-only implicates plan content as a candidate influence. If the placebo behaves
similarly, that weakens the attribution. All arms preserving the defining pursuit provides no
positive evidence for a destructive planning bottleneck on this case. All arms displacing it
leaves the writer/common task implicated but does not isolate its cause. One draw per condition
cannot estimate reliability or certify better prose. This stage's claim remains OBSERVED.

## B. Cross two inventor providers with two expander providers

Draw two further new 2048-bit integers; encode through unchanged invention-seed.v3. Each block
shares its seed across its two inventors and four expansions. Use exactly the blind mechanics

selection or favorable-source filtering. Native request objects name no model, allowing identical

opus-opus-1, opus-astra-1, opus-opus-2, opus-astra-2, astra-astra-2, astra-opus-2.
All mechanics precede all expansions. An invalid mechanics object skips its two descendants
without substitution. A reserved-name-invalid plan is retained as invalid; it has no descendant.

Compare exact operation, operative restrictions, acquisition and extensions across the four
designs. Within each parent, locate whether each expander retains, changes or discards those
mechanics, and which pursuits, choices, conflicts and additional dependencies it invents.
Record the matrix of observed transitions, not a single winner or an originality rate.

If different designs converge specifically through one expander, that locates a downstream
candidate influence. If the designs already converge, no expander can be blamed for inventing
their shared core. If the same design produces different stories under different expanders,
that shows source mechanics do not fully determine the story. If differences survive both,
preserve that limited transfer result. Two blocks and one draw per cell cannot establish a
universal provider property, training cause, diversity rate or production qualification.
This stage's claim remains OBSERVED, even if it identifies a useful direction.
