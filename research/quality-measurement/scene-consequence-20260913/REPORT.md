# Scene consequences: a partial repair in one fresh opening

The registered run completed six accepted chapters. The clearest defect in the
earlier opening is absent: the later test and repair both fit within the original
100 N allowance, so buying Load no longer becomes a prerequisite for obtaining
Duration. The failed repair also consumes material and work and interrupts the
crew's cooperation. Several exchanges give other participants interests that
survive Wren's explanation of the facts.

This is a partial editorial repair. Technical procedure still dominates the
opening, repeated qualifications remain, and an accessible test supplies the
missing Point in the very next chapter. Wren has more local emotional and social
responses, but her professional judgment still carries most choices. These are
located observations from reading every chapter in full, not literary scores or
a qualified reader result. One first output compared with a historical opening
cannot establish causal improvement, separate the bundled directions, or predict
general efficacy.

[Registration](RUNBOOK.md) and [frozen identities](registration.json) were committed
and pushed with the source changes in `12d99b8`, before calls. The runtime archived
base `52c2fdf` and overlaid only `application/outline.py` and `domain/house.py`.
The former routes concept planning through `planner.outline.v2` with directions
about consequential choices, participant aims and scene development. The latter
adds an explanation-limit instruction to named-viewpoint writing. Legacy planning
and other roles' general clarity floor retain their prior behavior. The original
seed, concept, six-scene layout, chapter target, native model/reasoning and verbatim

In the earlier opening, Chapter 6's 118 N test exceeded the starter allowance and
thus vindicated the supposed overinvestment in Load. The new opening keeps the
attraction of Load as a safety margin while removing that dependency. In Chapter 5
the catch prevents a fall but does not preserve the intended angle; the failed
bedding is not converted into an immediate successful repair. In Chapter 6 Wren
and the watcher develop a saddle with opposing wedges, and the longer hold works
with ordinary supports and curing material. Duration is useful, but this does not
prove it would have solved the earlier attempt alone or that buying Load was a
globally irrational decision.

The six chapters preserve the main progression: assessment and correction,
temporary crossing, negotiated shelter and work, a new test and Load purchase,
memorial relationship, failed bedding, then Duration and a supported permanent
repair. Familiar technical operations repeatedly occupy the foreground. New
materials and locations have not produced a broad change in dramatic rhythm.

There is also a new concern. Chapter 1, lines 193-207, spends the first cast on the
broken clipboard before the urgent water crossing. The tool is useful, but this
adds another small demonstration and weakens the intended first-use urgency. It
was absent from the outline; the subsequent chapters nevertheless carry its mana
cost correctly. This delivery should be retained as a counterexample to treating
the prompt changes as a comprehensive fix.

Expertise remains bounded: Wren corrects a material judgment in Chapter 1 and
accepts a contact-gap correction from the crew in Chapter 4. The permanent repair
depends on material and support after magic expires. The final fresh chalk mark
and Earth barcode establish a response while leaving the marker's identity and
ultimate purpose unresolved. The foreman's fate and Wren's arrival remain open;
the text does not impose an adopted homeward pursuit or close the serial.
