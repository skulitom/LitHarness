## Inputs and crossed conditions

Use one fresh project-authored harbour-rescue premise, constructed once before generation.
It is a research fixture, not an imported published story or a production narrative template.
Freeze a shared scene core, a shared short writer instruction, and an extended reference.
Core plus instruction must be at most 450 whitespace-delimited words; extended reference
must add at least 600. Those limits deliver the input manipulation, not a craft metric.
The core contains viewpoint, starting situation, immediate goal, abilities/resources and
their operational limits, and scene endpoint. All conditions target approximately 900 words.

The extra reference provides neutral character background, setting and later-book intentions.
It must not add a rule or prerequisite needed for the current rescue, prescribe a lesson,
or contradict the core. Mark it as reference, not compulsory on-page material. Source-review
this boundary before any call. Keeping biography out of a brief does not erase the character's
history; unseen reference facts are not grounds for penalizing the compact writer.

Generate one action plan from the full frozen source, without prior prose, comparisons,
criticism or experiment labels. Source-review the entire proposal for physical feasibility,
resource use and scope. Preserve its raw output. Only factual corrections are permitted,
recorded individually; a required narrative redesign, unresolved conflict or malformed
proposal stops the draft phase. Freeze the reviewed plan and all four requests before prose.

## Reading and claim boundaries

Read every complete draft in generation order. Locate introductory inventories, repeated
explanation, rhetoric that supplies a lesson after an event, concrete attempts and responses,
why the character chooses an action, resource/timing consistency, and endpoint realization.
Inspect loss of intelligibility or arbitrary action as well as retained mannerisms. Do not
infer quality from brevity, fewer metaphors, more dialogue, absence of one phrase, or adherence
alone. Account for the fact that the free condition can construct a different rescue.

Agent reading is defect harvest only. No quality scores, model ranking, candidate selection,
human panel, automatic editor, qualified reader mechanism or production gate is introduced.
Two draws in each of four cells on one new case cannot establish reliability, statistical
causality, a general writing ceiling or transfer across serial fiction. A changed plot or
opening inventory is manipulation delivery, not automatically better prose. Record bounded
observations and unresolved alternatives; keep conjecture distinct from evidence.

All narrative fixture data and prose remain under ignored runs. Commit identifiers, hashes,
derived counts, scoped observations and generic runner/tests. No original book, production
prompt, accepted plan or canon is changed by this diagnostic.
