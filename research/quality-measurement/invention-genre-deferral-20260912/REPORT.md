# Genre deferral: different premises, recurring expansion material

Deferring the LitRPG instruction did not meet the registered rule for a repeatable solution.
It produced several different initiating actions, and the preselected photographer story
retained its evidence-gathering pursuit through adaptation, planning and an opening chapter.
The other preselected deferred story already concerned restoring a community ferry. Both
deferred plans subsequently added nonhuman nursery obligations absent from their selected
premises and adaptations. Repetition also remained among the initial deferred outputs.

One early-genre plan coined the reserved skill name Standing. The existing validator refused
it, and its chapter was skipped without replacement. That leaves the second seed's chapter
comparison incomplete independently of the substantive recurrence. This is an OBSERVED
artifact result, not an originality rate, literary-quality finding or production promotion.

## What ran

Registration commit: `4cf9013b1185815df276dd7a5b555232b8a79b03`, committed and pushed before
dispatch. [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md) and [registration.json](registration.json) freeze the design;
[evidence.json](evidence.json) records the requests, controls, usage and artifact hashes.

Two fresh 2048-bit integers passed through the existing Base64 renderer. Within each seed,
early and deferred genre arms shared the same pursuit/action task, schema and prefix, and
each request ran twice in a fresh session. Only the initial genre sentence differed. Separate
precommitted selection integers selected item 2 for seed 1 and item 5 for seed 2, in both arms.
Repeat 1 supplied four chains. The same adaptation instruction, unchanged discovery planner
and common isolated chapter writer followed for each eligible source. No old story, critique,
reader answer, external example or candidate-selection model entered generation.

Nineteen calls completed: eight batches, four adaptations, four plans and three chapters.
One planned chapter was skipped after its parent's validation failure. All 48 initial premises,
four adaptations, four plans including the refused one, and three chapters were read after
the sequence finished. There was no transport stop, reroll, retry, fallback or resumed session.
This tests one generation procedure; the chapter writer is not the production book loop.

## The four selected chains

Paragraph numbers below count blank-line-separated paragraphs within the named field.
Draft paragraph numbers include displayed system messages and scene separators.

**Late seed 2: Nico, the photographer.** The initial item has an income motive, concealment
of a photograph, stolen cameras and an ownership dispute. Adaptation retains those actions
and adds a hotel dungeon, Memory Exposure and staff quests. The plan preserves evidence
gathering as the main pursuit, distinguishes memory images from independent records and
explains why an intact concealed film roll is not yet a usable photograph: it needs processing.
This is a located clarification, not a general coherence finding.

New material enters in `plan-late-2-1`, world paragraph 3 and growth paragraph 3: frost porters
have an ancestral nursery beneath the hotel, and cooperation for Nico's route-tracing power
depends on securing its access rights. Neither the selected premise nor its adaptation has
that nursery. The addition is not the entirety of this story: the sale investigation remains
central, and the chapter does not yet enact the nursery bargain.

The 1497-word chapter enacts the hidden roll, camera theft, first reconstructed image,
administrative exposure, medicine retrieval and an improved reconstruction. The original
desire to keep the studio remains at paragraphs 1, 3-8 and 68. Nico's choice to display
provenance has a consequence that the buyers exploit. This chain demonstrates that this
particular deferred premise can become usable magical action without replacing its main
investigation; it does not show superiority to its missing matched chapter control.

There is also a continuity slip: paragraphs 42-45 place Nico downstairs at the portrait
station; paragraph 64 labels his exposure location as a guest-room vestibule. The plan's
opening paragraph 5 had specified logging his room. This is a located conflict between the
drafted action and the displayed location, not proof of a general drafting mechanism.

**Late seed 1: Salma, the school ferry.** Restoring access, loss of relocation payments,
impoundment and a mobile school are already in the initial ordinary-world premise. Adaptation
adds pooled stamina, navigation advancement and the prospect of transporting buildings.
Planning retains those promises but adds amphibious reservoir administrators in ceramic
bodies, sustaining nursery settlements in their own world (`world:2`). Its later bargain
exchanges transport service for a platform and recognized landing rights (`growth:4`).

The 1475-word chapter delivers a proving run, consequential stamina sharing, withdrawal of a
donor, a useful route record, school reopening and the report that triggers relocation review.
It stops at the event boundary chosen by the plan; the later impoundment's absence is not
a missed chapter promise. The grandson in the initial and adapted premise is no longer named
in either plan or chapter. The school motive persists, but that particular family relationship
has fallen out of the developed material.

Operational explanations remain, for example paragraph 64 restates what the immediately
preceding exhaustion demonstrates. Ordinary narration also adds exact quantities such as
past years of employment and the duration of the crossing. These are located observations,
not scores or evidence that one arm has worse prose. The other arm uses a different story.

**Early seed 1: Imani, the interpreter.** Adaptation and planning retain refusal of imposed
treatment, cooperative signing, prisoner escape, sanctuary and a route home. The plan gives
the existing workers their own visual language and requires communication to be established;
the chapter follows that distinction instead of making Imani's signing universally understood.
An initial useful release precedes the mass unlocking, and Ward Gloss changes the escape route.

The same expansion tendencies are present in this control: world paragraph 3 introduces
ceramic kilnfolk, inherited body parts, family repair records and spawning kilns. Growth makes
sanctuary depend on negotiating household obligations at the pumping station. These additions
show that nonhuman kinship and infrastructure were not exclusive to either deferred plan.

The 1539-word chapter reaches the planned pumping-station refuge and keeps the portal-home
interest present. Its notebook is inconsistent: paragraph 10 takes the notebook from her jacket;
paragraph 51 places her notebook back on the consultation table she left.
The carried notebook reappears later. This is a discrete continuity defect in the retained
control, not a scored comparison with the deferred prose.

**Early seed 2: Mara, the shield.** Adaptation retains a suicidal wielder's unwanted surrender,
new hands, imperial confiscation and the threatened exchange of personhood. The plan develops
conflicting intentions: Tavin wants a hearing about his sister and later insists on permission
before Mara enters another plea. Porcelain clerks maintain distributed ancestors and pursue
their recognition as citizens (`world:4`), another addition of nonhuman family obligations.

The validation refusal concerns the proposed skill Standing in `growth:3`. It is capitalized
mid-sentence and explicitly named as a future power. This is not an ordinary lowercase use of
the word being mistaken for a name. `Discovery.from_invention` applied the existing
`schema_words.named_in` rule. The plan remains available for reading, but no chapter was
dispatched from it. The rule was neither changed nor bypassed for this experiment.

## Consequence for the next investigation

The most useful located boundary is now specific: both selected deferred premises retained
their basic causal pursuits, yet their detailed world/growth plans introduced nursery-related
obligations before prose. This does not identify which planning instruction causes the addition.
It argues for a matched test at that expansion boundary, using fixed sources and repeated
controls, before spending on another generic nonce or diversity wording change. The surviving
photographic investigation is a counterexample to total plot replacement, not a candidate
chosen as the best story or a reason to promote the whole procedure.
