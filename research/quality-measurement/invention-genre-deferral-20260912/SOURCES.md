# Basis and limits

The [active-seed result](../invention-active-seed-20260912/REPORT.md) did not establish a
consistent causal-story advantage from explicitly using the opaque prefix. The earlier
[premise-before-development pilot](../premise-before-development/RESULTS.md) changed call
count and framing together and did not establish an ordering effect. The present comparison
holds the pursuit task and subsequent stage count fixed while changing initial genre exposure.
These local artifacts motivate a question; neither is evidence that genre deferral works.

Related primary source: Yao et al., [Plan-and-Write](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05701v3),
AAAI 2019, abstract. It describes explicit storyline planning before story generation and
compares static with interleaved planning. This supports the existence of staged narrative
generation as a research approach. It does not test withholding LitRPG genre cues, opaque
Base64 seeds or the current subscription model. Its reported evaluation gains are not a
LitHarness quality qualification. No evaluation procedure or human label is imported here.
The abstract-page snapshot and local result hashes are recorded in sources.json. The publisher
snapshot download failed before any model call; the author-submitted arXiv page was captured.

The genre-deferral hypothesis and matched control are local conjectures. No article example,
external story, local generated story, diagnostic wording or prior critique enters a request.
The experiment adds no critic, ranking, probability estimate or rejection stage.
