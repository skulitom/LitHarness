## Remaining boundaries located by this run

**Reconciliation can promote speculation into a goal.** Neither the developed
Wren motive nor the Chapter 2 draft request supplies a homeward goal. Chapter 2
line 197 introduces a conditional thought about another arrival knowing a way
home. The grow call turns that into an accepted `wants` declaration: see
`book-1/calls/010.json`, bridge result 11, batch index 16 (zero-based), and
`rec-w9d12c13c32dbd41c25f771f0` at `s000002`. Chapter 3's accepted goal then returns
to inspection, shelter, the companion's chit and information about other arrivals
(`rec-w7cd501216299d10218a32349`, `s000003`). The home thought does not govern the
third chapter, but its intermediate promotion is real. This separates a remaining
draft/reconciliation path from the earlier concept-development motive change.

**The generated opening is deliberately absent from planning.** The fixed source
opening awards initial experience after the stair crossing; this plan omits that
award and places rank progression in later tests. Inspection of the actual
`book-1/calls/004.json` request and `Concept.for_outline` shows why the planner
does not receive that opening sequence: it removes `first_arc.opens`,
`discovery.opening`, `first_use` and `threat.first_reach`, while retaining world,
growth, later commitments and author decisions. This boundary predates the current
fixes. It is not evidence of accidental truncation, nor does this run establish
that reinstating the complete opening would improve the book.

**Repeated numerical investment remains untested.** The plan schedules Rank 1 and
Load investment in Chapter 4, then Rank 2 and Duration in Chapter 6. Wren stays
Rank 0 through this run. A maximum-one scale establishes ownership support; it
does not establish repeatable deepening. The existing scalar ceiling and the
source's repeatable point allocation remain a representation question. The earlier
explicit-depth replay control is separate evidence, not a replacement for a
continuation that actually spends and re-spends points.

The concrete next diagnostic is the goal-promotion boundary: distinguish a
conditional possibility from an adopted pursuit while retaining a positive case
where the character actually chooses the new goal. This is a proposed controlled
test, not a qualified interpretation rule or a production intervention.
