# Plot history at invention and expansion

The operator asked to search for better solutions to repeated story structures. This is an
isolated history-conditioning pilot, not production feedback or a literary quality gate.
Prior experiments varied nonces, templates, formats, batching and effort without eliminating
the recurring repertoire. The current CLI's distinct-from projection omits prior plot and
its instruction does not reach development. Those are implementation facts, not proved causes.

## Frozen history and generation arms

Use every premise from medium-1, medium-2 and medium-3 of invention-effort-20260911, in that
order: eighteen original first-response texts. Do not pick examples by their content, edit
them, summarize them with a model, or import report prose, dossiers or literature examples.
Freeze their receipts, individual hashes, exact history array and parent manifests. This
research registration authorizes this specific use of generated history only within this arm.

Draw two fresh 2,048-bit integers once, retaining their production v3 Base64 prefixes. Keep
the earlier frozen source at d4ebdb5, binary, medium effort, gpt-6-astra, system prompt, native
schema, six-premise count and 80-120-word task. The user prompt varies:

- baseline: no history, the existing ordinary batch request.
- identity: full history as inert reference data plus the existing novelty dimensions of
  protagonist, setting and core power; changing names or continuing an old world is insufficient.
- structure: the same full history and identity constraints, plus different initiating actions,
  causal sequences, ongoing pursuits and central conflicts. Cosmetic role/place substitutions
  do not satisfy it. No specific trope, name or setting is prohibited by the runner.

The identity arm is a history-and-ordinary-instruction control, not a literal reproduction
of CLI distinct-from: it receives complete short premises instead of world projections.
The structure arm has a longer instruction, so this does not isolate semantic wording from
additional instruction length. Shared fixed history also limits generalization across histories.

Generation order: baseline-1, identity-1, structure-1, structure-2, identity-2, baseline-2.
Before dispatch select one slot per block using
random.Random("invention-memory.v1:" + decimal_integer).randrange(6), shared by all three arms.
Retain the first draw and every output. No model ranks, rejects or selects candidates.

## Expansion and carry-through control

After all six batches, expand the selected premises in this order:
expand-baseline-1, expand-identity-1, expand-structure-1, retain-structure-1,
retain-structure-2, expand-structure-2, expand-identity-2, expand-baseline-2.

Every expand slot uses the unchanged discovery renderer, third person, selected premise only,
no writer or second prefix. Retain slots use the exact same selected premise and renderer,
but prepend the structural history instruction with an explicit requirement to preserve the
supplied premise's central action and pursuit while making added material distinct from the
history. This tests a practical combined instruction, not context length alone. The two
orders of clean/retained expansion reduce a simple order confound but cannot remove backend
variation. Hash each dependent request and its exact source before dispatch. Invalid parents
skip their expansions without substitute selection.

## Decision rule and evidence boundary

Inspect no new story prose until every registered slot completes or an operational stop fires.
Read all thirty-six premises and up to eight plans. Retain per-item hashes and lengths, plus
bounded paraphrases of initiating action, causal sequence and continuing pursuit/conflict.
Compare each arm with the frozen history and with the other new batches. Record counterexamples
and new repeated families as well as apparent differences. A name change or absent water word
alone is not success; literal searches may locate passages but supply no semantic score.

If structure reproduces the old causal sequences or only changes occupations/scenery, the
invention intervention fails here. If identity already changes them equally, do not credit
the added structural instruction. If distinct premises converge during clean expansion,
invention-only novelty is insufficient. If retained-history expansion still returns to the
old sequences, fails to preserve the selected premise, or merely replaces it with another
repeated family, the combined intervention is not a sufficient solution. A positive result
would be a limited feasibility lead, not qualification, quality, training distance, a general
novelty rate, or evidence identifying model priors/alignment/hidden context as the root cause.
