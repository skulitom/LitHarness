## Methodological conclusions

1. The isolated proposal-to-opening path can be executed and audited across every registered
   slot, subject to the documented hardware continuation and auditor correction.
2. The current design varies semantic content as well as representation. It compares whole
   proposal routes and does not identify a format-only effect.
3. Concept development is a model call, followed by mechanical replacement of
   `first_arc.opens` with `discovery.opening`. The outline projection then removes both,
   plus `first_use` and `threat.first_reach`. That source behavior is neither model
   preservation nor experimental evidence of a defect. Retained debts, turns and later
   commitments must be distinguished from raw opening fields when tracing transmission.
4. A single first-scene draft cannot adjudicate an experience deliberately scheduled later.
   A smaller on-page reward and a deferred full payoff also need separate descriptions.
5. An apparent preservation of plot purpose does not establish preservation of tone or
   interaction. These distinctions can guide a new research question; they are not labels
   for ranking, a craft metric, a production gate, or an established reader mechanism.
6. The six original briefs and one opening per route provide narrow genre and temporal
   coverage. They cannot establish long-serial retention of capability or popularity.

The local report proposes a separately scoped follow-up with common semantic inputs and an
explicit episode boundary, keeping representation and handoff contrasts separate. It has
not been launched. No specific research intervention is qualified by this record.
