## A missing distinction in the reconstruction target

The original world's Recall cost describes losing a bounded interval of memories by the
following morning. Its exact declaration value occurs in scene two's frozen system message.
The job-bound scene plan instead describes spending the intervening activity without the
memory operation, and the raw and accepted prose retain that less specific formulation.
Thus the operation was available to the writer; why it followed the plan's formulation is
not established. The historical plan lookup excludes later plan drift for this comparison.

Subsequent reconstruction inputs were extracted from the generated chapter. The cost unit
passes through `chapter.protected[27]`, `facts[39]` and `source.planner_units[39]` in the retained
protected-reconstruction, narration-obligations and source-contract preparation artifacts.
It became F28, whose unusual expenditure must remain legible.

Inspection of **all eight exact source-contract requests** found that they require this
unit, preserve supplied ambiguity and forbid canon repair. Their supplied cost does not
specify memory loss. The original operation is absent from both the systems and parsed
prompts. [inspection.json](inspection.json) pins every request and the located source spans;
lexical checks are supporting observations, not an automatic semantic-equivalence test.
The complete supplied passages were also inspected.

These calls validly test rendition of their declared protected draft. They do **not** test
restoration of the original cost, and their repeated expenditure explanations cannot rule
out a production repair restoring it. This qualifies the scope of the
[source-contract results](../prose-source-contract/RESULTS.md), without changing their
registration or observed outputs. The later disclosure-permission calls used a production
first-scene input and never reached this purchase; this qualification does not apply to
those calls by association.

This audit does not establish that the missing operation causes all redundant narration,
that a corrected plan will improve prose, or that all prior trials share this limitation.
Those would require distinct evidence. Future transfer claims should distinguish preserving
a generated draft from preserving the story's authoritative facts. A scoped comparison of
the original versus clarified scene-two cost is a conjecture, not a registered experiment.
