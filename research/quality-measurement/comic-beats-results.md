# The comic-beat census: where the genre runs, where we run, and how far the instrument can be trusted

**Status: MEASUREMENT, 2026-08-22. No bar is declared here, nothing is admitted to any registry,
no directive was authored, and nothing under `src/` moved.** Per `plan/reader-read-2.md` a counter
enters the registry by an operator act over a measured distribution. This is the distribution.
The admission, if there is one, is the operator's.

Every number below is reproducible from a committed results JSON carrying ids and numbers only,
under registration digest **`d3200ddad172e4854b70`**. `comic_beats.py --selftest` fails if the
frozen block moves, and every arm refuses to read a result file whose digest is not that one.

**Beats per 1,000 words is a density of *attempts*.** A chapter with more of them is not better.
Whether any of them lands is not asked, not schema'd, and not derivable from anything here.

## The headline, stated first because it is not the flattering one

**The genre attempts levity at a median of 5.50 beats per 1,000 words, Reappraisal sits at the
61st percentile of it — and the instrument that says so has a reliability of 0.54, so the same
chapter re-asked lands anywhere between the 39th and the 71st percentile.**

Two things follow, and they point in opposite directions.

The operator's question has an answer at the population grain and the answer refutes the premise
the work started from. This project does **not** attempt humor at zero while the genre runs at N.
Its published chapters attempt it slightly *above* the genre median, and above the 75th percentile
once chapter length is controlled for. Nothing here says those attempts land. It does say the
absence this census went looking for is not there.

The same question has **no** answer at the chapter grain. A byte-identical re-ask moves the count
by a median of 5 beats; the two answers agree on 18% of the spans they locate; and 46% of the
observed spread across 236 published chapters is the instrument rather than the chapters. Of the
two pre-registered readings that would have licensed the placement, both miss.

## 4. Q3 — whether the locator is an instrument

Every control's number, and every reading that did not fire publishes the interval it could not
exclude. `INSIDE_NOISE` and `DOES_NOT_SEE` are never reported alone, because a noisy instrument
passes a null automatically (§101.1).

### The noise floor: `repeat`

The same chapter, the same request, a distinct sample index — `n` byte-identical requests, so the
variation is the model's own sampling and not a perturbed prompt.

| substrate | pairs | median \|Δ\| | mean | max | median \|Δ\|/1k | anchor overlap (mean) |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| RoyalRoad | 58 | **5** | 5.91 | 30 | 2.54 | **0.176** |
| own | 12 | **5** | 4.83 | 15 | 4.79 | **0.110** |

A re-ask moves the count by a third of itself, and the two answers agree on roughly one located
span in six. **The count is noisy and the locations barely replicate at all.**

### The same-text control, and it was not registered

`strip_placebo` asks for an inert revision (fix any typos) and the writer mostly returned the
chapter unchanged: on our own substrate **9 of 11** placebo revisions are byte-identical to their
original and all 11 normalise identically. Because each arm holds its own cache file, those calls
were re-issued rather than replayed — so the placebo count is a second draw on the same text.

| substrate | units same after normalisation | byte-identical | median \|Δ\| | max |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| own | 11 | 9 | **8** | 19 |
| RoyalRoad | 3 | 1 | 7 | 8 |

`toll-road:scene-1` returned **10 beats one time and 22 the other on the same characters.**

**This was not declared before the run and it does not replace the `repeat` arm.** It is reported
beside it because it is the least deniable form of the same measurement — not a byte-identical
*request* reached through a sample index, but a byte-identical *chapter* reached through a
different arm — and because it agrees with the registered control rather than rescuing anything.

### What the noise costs Q1: the variance decomposition

The `repeat` arm measures the sd of the difference between two draws on the same chapter, which is
`sqrt(2)` times the sd of one measurement's error. The population's observed variance then splits
with no free parameters. It assumes the error is homoscedastic across chapters and independent
between draws; neither is checked.

| | RoyalRoad (58 pairs) | own (12 pairs) |
|---|--:|--:|
| sd of the paired difference | 4.22 | 5.55 |
| **sd of one measurement's noise** | **2.99** | 3.92 |
| sd of the population, observed | 4.39 | 4.65 |
| sd of the population, implied true | 3.21 | 2.49 |
| **reliability** | **0.537** | 0.287 |
| ceiling on any correlation this measure could show | 0.733 | 0.536 |
| draws per chapter needed to reach reliability 0.8 | **4** | 10 |
| draws to reach 0.9 | 8 | 23 |

**Q1's distribution is real and it is wider than the truth.** Roughly 46% of the observed spread
across published chapters is the instrument. The correlation ceiling is the number a later
programme should be told before it tries to relate this measure to anything: no correlate of
single-draw located levity can exceed 0.73, whatever is on the other side.

### The layout sham

`ablate.rewhitespace` at full strength: not one character of any word changes. Read against the
`repeat` spread on the same chapters, one-sided.

| substrate | verdict | aligned | p | k needed | **median (sham − repeat), 90% CI** |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|---|
| RoyalRoad | `INSIDE_NOISE` | 26/51 | 0.500 | 32 | **0.0 beats [−1.0, +1.0]** |
| own | `INSIDE_NOISE` | 5/10 | 0.623 | 9 | 0.0 beats [−2.0, +1.0] |

**§78's failure mode is excluded, and tightly.** 26 of 51 is the coin exactly. Reflowing every

broken", because sampling variance has a known fix and a layout artifact does not.

mandatory, because without it a drop in located beats is equally well explained by the fact of a
rewrite.

**The manipulation is certified and the certificates say so.** No null below can be blamed on a
revision that did not happen:

declared obliquely and carries no multiplicity policy. That is a defect in the pre-registration
and it is named rather than exploited.** `PRE_REGISTRATION.declared_quantities.strip_drop` says
the drop is "read twice" — against the placebo and against the noise floor — and both of those are
`DOES_NOT_SEE`. `prose_kinds_only` tests the sentence in
`PRE_REGISTRATION.strip_internal_control` ("prose kinds fall and `system_voice` does not"), so it
is pre-registered in substance, but the declaration never named a primary reading and never
declared a correction for reading the arm three ways. At a Bonferroni-adjusted 0.0167 for three
readings, p=0.039 does not clear. **The conservative reading is the one this document adopts: on
RoyalRoad the strip arm points the right way and the design could not resolve it from zero.**

A second inconsistency, printed rather than resolved in the flattering direction: on RoyalRoad the
`against_placebo` median interval is [+2.0, +4.0] and **excludes zero**, while its sign test reads
p=0.067. The two disagree because the declared tie policy drops zero differences from the sign
test's denominator and the interval keeps them. The sign test is the declared statistic and it is
the one the verdict comes from.

**Refusal states and the internal control.**

- On our own substrate the strip arm is **`VOID`**, by its own pre-registered refusal rule: the
  median drop is exactly 0.0 beats against a confabulation rate of 0.375 beats per unit, and an
  effect the instrument's own noise could manufacture is not an effect. The rule fired as
  declared; it was not written afterwards to explain a null.
- On RoyalRoad it is `READABLE`: median drop 6.0 beats against 0.708 confabulated beats per unit.
- The `system_voice` internal control is **`CONTROL_HOLDS` at n=2** on RoyalRoad, which is a floor
  and not a pass — RoyalRoad's system voice is not written in this project's `**bold**` and
  `[STATUS]` typography, so only two chapters in the subset had a protected span at all. On our
  own substrate it is **`CONTROL_UNDECIDED`**: across 11 pairs the `system_voice` count *fell* on
  1 and *rose* on 6, which is the same undirected noise as everything else and is reported as
  neither a hold nor a failure.

## 5. What the instrument is, mechanically

One model call per chapter, `claude-haiku-4-5` over the `claude -p` transport, one whole chapter
per call. The system block, the question, the schema and the seven kinds are byte-frozen and
content-addressed; `--selftest` fails unless `registration_digest()` still equals
`d3200ddad172e4854b70`, and every arm refuses a result file carrying a different one. That is
`llm-reader-engagement.md` §A1's rule with a check on it — T0's A4 put about fourteen points of a
verdict on wording, so a reworded prompt is a different instrument with no evidence behind it.

**The asking carries no quality vocabulary, and the selftest enforces it as a property rather than
an intention.** `funny`, `humour`, `comedy`, `joke`, `good`, `works`, `lands`, `effective`,
`successful`, `quality` and `better` are searched for in the rendered system block, question,
schema and kind definitions, and the selftest fails if any appears. The brief's draft definition
of `system_voice` read "arch, petty, or funny"; the last word is a verdict and this instrument
says "theatrical".

**Every beat is checked against the page.** The model returns an anchor of at most twelve words
and a kind from a closed set of seven. The anchor is counted only if it is findable in the text
*that call was shown*, by normalised substring match — NFKC, curly quotes and dashes folded to
ASCII, whitespace collapsed, casefolded. An unfindable anchor is a confabulation: excluded from
every count and reported as a rate on every arm. That deterministic check is the one advantage
this locator has over E6's frozen matchers, which could score the model's vocabulary but never the
model against the text. Measured: **2.6% of RoyalRoad anchors and 5.6% of ours are confabulations**,
and 4 of the 3,086 anchors RoyalRoad returned carried a kind outside the closed set.

A second, deliberately looser match — all non-alphanumeric characters dropped — is computed and
reported as `relaxed_only` and never enters a count. **65 of the 80 RoyalRoad confabulations would
have matched under it**, so the strict rule is costing about four-fifths of its rejections to
punctuation the model retyped. It stays strict: loosening a rule after reading the answers is a
rubric fitted to its own answers, which is what freezing `AXIS_MATCHERS` exists to prevent one
module over.

Whitespace collapse is what makes the sham arm scoreable: `rewhitespace` changes no character of
any word, so an anchor found in the reflowed text is findable in the original by the same rule.
The selftest pins that.

**Statistics.** Every declared reading is an exact one-sided sign test, enumerated rather than
approximated, with its attainable floor (`1/2**G`) and the `k` it needed printed beside the `p` it
achieved. One-sided because every alternative is directional and was declared so before the run;
§89's two-sided reasoning was invariance under a sign flip of a *fitted* direction and nothing
here is fitted. A zero difference leaves the denominator. Below G=5 the outcome is
`INSUFFICIENT_N`, which is a different outcome from a failure and is never converted into one.
Every reading that does not fire publishes a distribution-free 90% interval on the median it did
not move.

**Substrate, and what is committed.** RoyalRoad text crosses from the MirrorBench interpreter to
this one as a gitignored JSONL under `derived/`; the committed results carry ids and numbers only,
and a RoyalRoad anchor is stored as an offset and a hash and never as a quoted string. Own-
generated anchors are stored verbatim, because we own that prose. Every revision this module
generated — on either substrate — is under `derived/` too: a revision of our own chapter is prose
rather than a number. `corpus_leak_audit.py` reports CLEAN across all 231 commits.

## 6. Known residuals, unfixed and named

- **The kind is the model's and nothing checks it.** Only the anchor is verified. §1's first row
  marks the chapter's opening clause `callback`, which it is not. The kind mix in §2 describes
  what the locator said; the density is the only number the findability check stands behind.
- **The answers are not in text order** despite the instruction — that same `callback` sat at
  offset 0 and arrived twenty-fourth. Offsets are recorded, so nothing here depends on the order
  the model returned.
- **Granularity is the model's too.** A run of banter can be marked once or three times; the
  twelve-word anchor limit bounds a beat from above and not from below. This is the largest
  unmodelled source of variance in the count and the `repeat` arm is the only thing that prices
  it.
- **Deliberation depth is the transport's, not a parameter of this instrument.** `claude -p` runs
  with thinking on and most of each answer's output tokens are thinking tokens — which is also the
  dominant term in the price. It is constant across every arm, so no comparison here is confounded
  by it, and no number here is comparable to a run made with a different local install.
- **Nine of 249 chapters were lost to the transport, and the losses are not random.** Four could
  not be sent (command line at or past 32,767 characters) and four timed out at the 300-second
  ceiling. Both mechanisms are length-correlated, and the timeout is additionally *beat-count*
  correlated: a chapter with more located beats generates more thinking and takes longer, so the
  censoring runs against the top of the distribution. Reappraisal chapter 2 timed out twice at
  three workers and returned at one. The pooled p95 and max are therefore mild underestimates.
- **`system_voice` cannot be stripped, by construction**, since the revision contract copies
  protected spans byte-for-byte. That is what makes it the strip arm's internal control rather
  than a gap in it — but the arm has never tested whether the locator can see a System voice being
  removed, and on RoyalRoad the control has n=2.
- **The pre-registration did not name a primary strip reading or a multiplicity policy.** Three
  readings of one arm at α=0.05 carry a family-wise error near 0.14. §4 adopts the conservative
  reading; a repeat of this arm should declare one primary reading before it runs.
- **The familiarity term is named and not measured.** RoyalRoad text may be memorised by the
  locator. The two substrates are never pooled and the `strip` differential on one chapter
  partially cancels the term, but nothing here estimates its size. Suggestively, the noise floor
  is *lower* on RoyalRoad (overlap 0.176) than on our own prose (0.110), which is the direction
  familiarity would predict and is equally well explained by RoyalRoad's shorter chapters.
- **A hand-count is not ground truth and no hand-count exists here.**
  `opening-counters-results.md` established that a human count is one more instrument with its own

## 7. What was deliberately not built

- **No landing measurement.** Whether a beat lands is valence; valence is behavioural or it is

- **No inject arm.** A model adding jokes is what a later programme would study, not a control for
  this one.
- **No bounce or punch-up role.** It is out of scope by the brief and it stays out: it should be
  designed only if a census shows a gap and clears Q3, and this census shows no gap and does not
  clear Q3.
- **Nothing reaches a prompt, a directive, or any generation path.** No axis, no `COUNTERS` entry,
  no directive kind, no Director brief vocabulary, no persona reason code, no writer dossier. A
  directive about humor, if one is ever authored, carries this census's number the way C8 carries
  the sentence-length number — and is authored by the operator.
