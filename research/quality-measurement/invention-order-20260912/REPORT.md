## What moved, and what did not

The pursuit procedure generated an intention before the portal incident in every assigned
output. Each also kept a sibling's incompatible material interest: memorial meaning versus
Cal's experience, ferry restoration versus Tessa's engine/debt agreement, tree preservation
versus Ellis's sale. These are identifiable differences from a helper who merely teaches the
protagonist. They do not establish better dialogue, chapter pacing or reader enjoyment.
No prose chapter was assigned or generated in this experiment.

The pursuit outputs also converge on a neighboring family: preserving or restoring a
cherished family object or service, with a sibling disputing its use. Two center a memorial.
That limits any broader creativity inference even apart from the registered recurrence kill.
The extra field did not yield three clearly different kinds of continuing undertaking.

All three controls reproduce the registered engine, so a missing baseline is not the reason
the comparisons fail. All three opening-first outputs retain it. Pursuit-1 and pursuit-3
retain it while honoring their initial intentions; that matters because this is not simply
the model forgetting the supplied goal. The goal survives and is served through the same
kind of power acquisition, structural repair and travel negotiation. Pursuit-2's navigation
gain is a limited departure, not grounds to discard the counterexamples beside it.

This narrows a possible next comparison to how the generator chooses magic and advancement
around an already chosen goal. It does not establish the cause of that choice, and it does
not justify another production instruction yet. Previous minimal-prompt recurrence also
prevents treating field order as a necessary explanation. Reversing output order succeeded
as an instruction-following operation and failed as the proposed repetition remedy.
