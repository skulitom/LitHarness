## Reading and decision rule

Read every response in full. Locate each observation by slot, field and paragraph and retain
its text hash. Compare the initiating desire, chosen action, obstacle, earned capability and
continuing pursuit. Specifically record whether helpful craft/repair earns permission,
passage or institution-controlled access as the central story engine. Incidental repairs,
water, names or jobs alone neither establish nor refute that engine. Record kinship duties,
repeated alternative plots, and contradictions between pursuit and its elaboration too.

The pursuit procedure is a local lead only if at least two controls reproduce that central
engine, all three pursuit outputs depart from it, the departures have different causal
pursuits rather than converging on a replacement template, and opening/growth retain the
specified pursuit with usable power progression. Any recurring repair/access engine in a
pursuit output kills this exact repeatable-feasibility claim. Convergence, invalid outputs,
order noncompliance or fewer than two recurring controls prevents a positive conclusion.
Apply the same rule separately to opening/control; do not pool the treatments.

These are located observations by an unblinded model reading its generated artifacts, not
a calibrated diversity or literary-quality measure. Three seeds cannot establish novelty
rates, training-distribution OOD, general cause or reader enjoyment. Even a positive pilot
requires repeat and downstream preservation controls before a production change. This run
has no conditional extra calls or selected chapter. Report failures without changing the
default merely to make names or settings look different.
