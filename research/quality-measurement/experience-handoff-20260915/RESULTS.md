## Existing production path: a source-code distinction

The research adapter deliberately sends the full specification only to discovery, then
passes the short premise as `author_brief` at later stages. B restores the original
specification at outline. This withholding belongs to the registered research design.

The production CLI already passes its original brief to discovery and concept development,
stores that same text as `Concept.author_brief`, and retains it in `Concept.for_outline`.
The projection removes generated opening choreography, not the original brief. See
[CLI](../../../src/litharness/cli.py) and
[concept](../../../src/litharness/application/concept.py); the exact tested source hashes
are in [registration.json](registration.json).

Therefore this run does not identify a missing production field or license redundant new
handoff machinery. Using an experience specification through the existing original-brief
channel is a candidate next test. That route would expose the specification at different
stages from this experiment and has not been run here. Choosing attractive intentions
autonomously is also separate from preserving a supplied intention.
