# Isolating premise preservation during expansion

The operator accepted the preservation-only lead from invention-memory-20260911. That pilot
combined history, structural contrast and an explicit instruction to preserve the selected
premise. This registered ablation compares preservation alone with clean and combined
expansion. It is isolated research, not an editorial policy or production feedback loop.

## Fixed inputs and conditions

Use both previously preselected structural-arm premises, unedited: structure-1 item 5 and
structure-2 item 2 from invention-memory-20260911. Keep the original selection, including the
counterexample; do not choose further stories by content or invent replacement premises.
The sources have already been observed, so this is a test on known examples, not transfer.
Freeze their receipts, selected-item hashes and the parent's registered eighteen-item history.
Generated history is authorized as reference data only within this isolated experiment.
No summaries, dossiers, reader responses or literature prose enter a request.

All conditions use the parent's frozen source at d4ebdb5, native binary, requested gpt-6-astra,
medium effort and unchanged discovery renderer, third person, with no writer. They receive
the exact same premise. There is no new invention or additional Base64 prefix at expansion;
the source premises retain their provenance from the earlier seeded invention.

- clean: the original expansion request with no added instruction or history.
- preserve: prepend exactly "Preserve the supplied premise's central action and pursuit.
  Do not replace the supplied premise with another story." to the clean user prompt.
- combined: the exact previous retained-history expansion request, including full history,
  structural contrast and the full CARRY instruction. Its extra middle sentence asks for
  added material distinct from previous premises.

Preserve removes both history and the history-dependent contrast sentence from combined.
It does not isolate the two removed components from each other. Clean versus preserve also
differs in instruction length; there is no length-matched placebo in this small ablation.

## Repeats and fixed order

Make two fresh-session calls per premise and condition, twelve total. Request bytes are
identical across the two repeats of a condition and premise. Do not add a nonce, tune a
request after dispatch, retry, reject, rank or replace any result.

1. Premise 1, repeat 1: clean, preserve, combined.
2. Premise 2, repeat 1: preserve, combined, clean.
3. Premise 1, repeat 2: combined, preserve, clean.
4. Premise 2, repeat 2: clean, combined, preserve.

Reversing each premise's order balances pairwise precedence within that premise. It does
not balance every condition equally across absolute positions or remove temporal/backend
variation. The two premises are the source units; repeated plans are not independent sources.

## Inspection and decision rule

Do not inspect new generated prose until all slots finish or an operational stop fires.
Read every first response, compare it with its exact source, and retain bounded paraphrases
of the opening's consequential power use, its earned advancement, ongoing pursuit and added
world/conflict material. Audit hashes, request equality, provenance and launch configuration.
Literal searches may locate passages but do not constitute semantic scores or quality labels.

For the electrician, examine whether the offensive auction escape and programmable rebel
craft remain causal drivers. Network control is already in the premise; its later presence
alone is not an expansion defect. For the inventory inventor, examine the aircraft,
component retrieval, transmitter/daughter pursuit and rewarded machine hunters. Record
whether an inserted unrelated rescue or service project takes over the opening's advancement.
Water already belongs to the offshore pursuit; absence of water is not a success criterion.

Compare all clean repetitions before crediting an improvement to preserve. A single favorable
plan is insufficient. A repeatable feasibility lead must survive both repeats and both
premises beyond the variation visible in clean plans. A difference limited to one premise
is source-specific. If preservation retains the central plot but familiar additions persist,
it is not sufficient to solve repetition. If combined behaves similarly to preserve, this
small study cannot prove equivalence or that history never matters. Report counterexamples
and uncertainty rather than declaring a novelty rate, quality gain, general cure or root cause.
No world-building run or drafted chapter is included, so there is no downstream transfer claim.
