# Preservation alone does not establish a repetition fix

The recoverable responses do not establish a consistent improvement from the preservation-only
instruction. Both clean and preservation-only expansions keep the selected central plots.
Their additions vary, and preservation alone still introduces the familiar rescue, nursery,
repair and inherited-community material. The two recovered combined-history inventor plans
keep his own engineering task central to opening advancement, a narrower observation that
does not establish a general fix or isolate history from structural contrast.

## Execution and the shutdown loss

The original protocol was committed as `87f7607` before dispatch. It planned two repeats
of clean, preserve and combined expansion for each of two fixed premises. Those premises
were the earlier history pilot's preselected electrician and inventory inventor, unedited.
The preserved instruction adds only a requirement to keep the central action and pursuit
and not replace the supplied story. Combined repeats the earlier full-history instruction.
Clean and combined requests exactly reproduce their parent experiment's prepared requests.

The machine overheated after four call slots had been created. The first two completed
receipts survived. The progress file, two later receipts and two transport records contained
only zero bytes after the shutdown. No final response file was recoverable from the remaining
temporary transport directory. Those artifacts were preserved, hashed and left unchanged.
The lost slots are combined-1-1 and preserve-2-1; their output, outcome and usage remain unknown.

Following the operator's explicit continuation request, amendment `8747711` was committed
before any further dispatch or inspection of new story prose. It continued only the eight
unstarted slots in the original order. No lost slot was retried or replaced. The resumed
phase wrote separate receipts and progress using flushed file data and atomic replacement.
This is an operational change, not a prompt change or a hardware-durability guarantee.

All eight resumed calls completed with valid shapes, using 54,912 recorded tokens. The two
intact original calls used 12,197 tokens. The recoverable total is therefore 67,109 tokens,
plus unknown usage from the two lost slots. The original twelve-attempt limit was preserved;
the original 100,000-token limit cannot be verified. The separately registered resumed phase
stayed below its 65,000-token stop. No new failure, retry, replacement or frozen-file drift
occurred. All ten recoverable first responses were read after the resumed sequence finished.

Captured requests, selected-premise provenance, native configuration and medium effort match
the registration. The ten recoverable calls have distinct sessions and output texts. Four
complete repeat pairs have identical captured input fields; two pairs cannot be checked
because a receipt is missing. Uncached plus cached input-token totals still differ between
identical captured repeats: clean-1 reports 4,350 versus 4,344, and combined-2 reports 6,721
versus 6,719. These differences are unexplained by capture. Full backend context, resolved
model and sampling distribution remain unreported; local equality is not proof of complete
backend identity.

## What the comparisons do and do not show

For Pavel, both clean repeats and both preservation-only repeats retain the auction escape
and programmable rebel craft. One preserve opening omits the helper detour and escapes with
a useful docket; the other frees a courier and bargains for help. The shorter detour is not
a repeated difference across the two preserve calls. Both clean plans already keep the
planned offensive action central, so its survival alone cannot be credited to the added
instruction. Nursery, ancestral-component and service-network material remains in several
variants, including preserve and combined.

Network politics is already explicit in Pavel's source premise. Its presence is therefore
not itself an expansion error. The recurring additions concern the invented inhabitants,
ancestral property, care tasks and negotiations placed around that conflict. Nor do these
plans become the same complete story merely because such material recurs.

For Benji, the surviving preservation-only plan contains the flood and nursery detour
described above. It still preserves the main aircraft, transmitter and daughter pursuit,
and it awards the opening's slot for transporting the radio. The nursery rescue is an
access detour, not the event that directly earns that slot. A later repair supplies another
advancement opportunity. This distinction prevents overstating the recurrence.

The clean controls vary in the same comparison: one explicitly advances through a buyer
rescue; the other advances through hauling equipment toward the radio, with escape for other
customers as a consequence. Thus own-purpose equipment use is also possible without the
preservation sentence. The two combined-history repeats both advance through Benji's own
crossing or hauling task. They still add family searches, repair bargains or restored routes
around later growth. Their dry obstacles do not by themselves demonstrate novelty; offshore
rescue is part of the original premise, and water's presence or absence is not the criterion.

The original requirement for both repeats and both premises cannot be fully evaluated:
one combined repeat for Pavel and one preservation-only repeat for Benji were lost. Do not
describe this as a completed balanced experiment. The restart also creates a temporal break.
All comparisons use two already-observed source premises and one fixed history; there is no
fresh-premise transfer, chapter drafting, reader-response or literary-quality measurement.

## Decision

Do not change production defaults on this result. The added preservation sentence has not
established an advantage beyond the clean controls' variation, and a visible counterexample
shows it is insufficient to remove recurring surrounding material. This is a limited result
on the recoverable outputs, not proof that preservation instructions never help.

The combined instruction retains a source-specific lead for Benji's opening. It still bundles
history, structural contrast and preservation, with additional context and instruction length.
It neither establishes that history is necessary nor solves the recurring surrounding
repertoire. No production prompt, seed, selector or database was changed. The user-requested
opaque Base64 seeding default remains in place.
