# Active Base64-prefix use: observed, insufficient in this pilot

Explicitly asking the model to use the random prefix did not meet the registered
feasibility rule. The active outputs contain some distinct premises, but also repeatedly
use professional support powers, rescues that transfer danger, and growing obligations
to communities. The general-diversity and passive controls contain departures too.
Both active repeats for the second seed begin with a midwife saving young dependents
through a transfer power. One selected active expansion retains a distinctive theatrical
escape, while the other develops nursery history and sanctuary repairs around infant care.

All eighteen first attempts completed, with seventy-two structurally valid premises and
six valid expansions. The claim remains **OBSERVED**, without a qualified semantic measure
or production change. This is a negative feasibility result for this exact adaptation,
not a refutation of every use of seeds or proof about the model's training distribution.

## Registered inputs and verified capture

binary, requested gpt-6-astra at medium effort and subscription authentication. Calls ran
sequentially with fresh sessions, no tools, retries, API fallback or shared story history.
New prose remained unread until the whole sequence finished. Every premise and plan was
then read in full.

Two fresh 2048-bit integers produced two opaque prefixes using the existing v3 renderer.
Each prefix was held fixed across passive, diverse and active, including the second repeat.
Diverse and active share a diversity instruction and final-only output request; one

Separate random integers preselected position 5 for block 1 and position 1 for block 2,
before outputs. Only repeat-1 positions were expanded, in all three arms, using unchanged
discovery and the exact supplied premise. Repeat-2 outputs were retained and inspected.
The model did not rank, filter or choose a candidate.

## Comparisons that determine the result

Active-1-1's first premise transfers a school's weight onto a sacred moon; active-1-2's
first transfers pressure to save a ferry and drains a noble's reservoir. The settings and
mechanics differ, but both use a retired maintenance worker named Fen, a rescue imposing
an elite's loss, a sabotage accusation and repair-based route expansion. Stable characters
under a repeated seed are not themselves a failure; the relevant recurrence is the familiar
causal family, also present in diverse-1-2's nursery-weight transfer and other controls.

Across the two active seeds, dependency and displaced costs remain prominent. The first
seed's Sada borrows a newborn's future to rescue a maternity ward; the second seed's Mara
borrows breath to rescue an infant and incurs a patron's custody claim. In the second
repeat, Wen again acts as a midwife, transfers dangerous force away from a nursery and
acquires delivery obligations. Renaming the patients as storms does not remove the care,
transfer and disputed-burden structure. Luis's postponed collapse, UNXY's sacrificed
loaves and Ivo's erased reef offer further active variants of displaced danger.

Departures matter too. The octopus's liberated storms, the vending machine's memory
commerce, Nell's murder investigation, Jori's theatrical escape and the silkworm's
counterfeit citizenship are not one identical story. They do not consistently exceed
both controls across the blocks and repeats. Passive Nessa's accidental killing leads
to corrective exploration; diverse Davi's ink consumption awakens a giant; diverse
Arturo negotiates dungeon bouts; Rook's escape and distance theft supply another pursuit.
The general-diversity arm itself still returns to nursery rescue and infrastructure
dependence. Neither instruction is established as sufficient by this inventory.

An explicitly post-observation spot check found that UNXY, the organism identifier in
active-1-1 item 6, exactly matches seed 1's characters at zero-based slice `[334:338]`.
Among the checked names Fen, Nox, Qelbiw, Prar, UNXY, Jori, Mara, Wen, Ivo, Seli and Sera,
only UNXY occurs in its corresponding prefix, even ignoring case. This is a literal
overlap between saved inputs and outputs, not proof of how the model used the seed.
That same premise still rescues a child by transferring harm into communal food. Token
reuse or an unfamiliar identifier is not the registered causal-departure criterion.

## Interpretation and limits

This pilot does not justify making the active-use sentence a default novelty mechanism.
It also does not show that the prefix is ignored: the intervention changes instructions,
and one observed identifier literally overlaps its seed. It supplies no measurement of
attention to all characters, no deterministic story-to-seed mapping and no actual sampler
control. The repetition issue remains unresolved.

Two seed units, dependent items within each batch, one expansion per selected repeat-1
source, unblinded reading and unknown backend behavior constrain inference. There are no
repeated expansions, no shared-premise treatment comparison, no chapter drafts and no
qualified literary-quality or population-novelty measure. Full probability calibration,
model-generated strings and explicit reasoning transcripts were not tested. See
[SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) for the precise adaptation and primary references; this result
does not refute the full String Seed of Thought method.
