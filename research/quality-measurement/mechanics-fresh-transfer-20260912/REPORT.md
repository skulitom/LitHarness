# Acquisition transfers; fresh story diversity does not

The registered separate mechanics step preserves exercise-based acquisition in all three
fresh plans and the preassigned staged chapter. It does **not** solve repetition. All three
independent mechanics outputs describe closely related heat-transfer disciplines before any
character, goal or setting is supplied. The resulting plans again organize progress around
repair, route information and access; the staged chapter repairs a ferry for passage and
ends with another maintenance stop.

The narrow acquisition-transfer claim is **SUPPORTED by this test**. The broad consistent
repetition-avoidance claim is **REFUTED** by its registered recurrence and replacement-engine
kills. This is no literary-quality qualification. Production defaults remain unchanged.

and staged-3 learns specifically to recover the ferry and its cargo. Those surrounding
dependencies are part of the broad failure, not evidence of independence from repair work.

The staged plans retain their source's operative limits and later extensions. Supplying a
mechanic can successfully constrain invention while failing to diversify it. In particular,
staged-3's shell keepers and hatching-pool dispute are newly invented during discovery; its
mechanics source contains no life, family, settlement or route.

## Decision and next discriminating question

Keep the narrow acquisition result as a bounded preservation result, not a diversity fix.
Do not promote separate magic design as the default on this evidence. The fresh mechanics
distribution itself is now the clearest localized problem to test: the pipeline faithfully
preserves a component that was already repetitive when invented.

A useful next contrast would change the mechanics generator's provider family while holding
the downstream discovery request fixed, then test whether different first components remain
different through planning. That would distinguish failure to invent varied components from
failure to preserve them. The earlier [provider comparison](../invention-cause-20260910/REPORT.md)
is a reason to investigate that boundary, not proof that another provider solves it. No
provider change or additional generation is made by this result record.

Separately, [Narrative Keyframing, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10337v1), introduction and
system-design sections, describes character-specific first-person narratives as intermediate
material for later third-person prose, with interactive author selection. That suggests a
different characterization experiment, but it does not establish fresh-start diversity in
LitHarness or validate an automated selector. The paper was consulted during this frozen run;
none of its prose or examples entered any generation request.
