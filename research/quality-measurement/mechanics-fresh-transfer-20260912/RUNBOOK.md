## Question and boundaries

The preceding mechanics-isolation experiment changed first-power acquisition on three supplied
preservation/restoration goals. All six staged plans retained designed acquisition exercises,
while direct plans invented service for another inhabitant as the qualification. Its broader
staging and withholding feasibility rules were not met: later repair/access obligations and
similar heat-transfer designs remained. See that experiment's content-addressed records;
this is a transfer test, not an assumption that staging fixed creativity.

Now ask whether the same mechanics-design procedure changes acquisition when the story starts
without any supplied goal, and whether that survives into a preassigned opening chapter.
Also inspect convergence on calibration exercises, technical support powers, or a new repeated
plot. No new instruction to avoid those things is added. These are three seed blocks with one
draw per condition, and only one downstream chapter pair. They cannot establish broad literary
quality, out-of-distribution generation, or the cause of model-level repetition.

## Reading and decision rules

Read no new story material until all eleven slots finish or an operational stop occurs.
Then read all outputs in full, including invalid ones. Locate observations by slot, field
and paragraph and retain receipt/text hashes and exact request lineage. Read both chapter
texts completely; retain missing acquisition or first use as missing coverage, not success.

Trace the invented pursuit and conflict, first acquisition, first use, later advancement,
and newly introduced dependencies. Repairs, water and names alone are not the target engine:
it consists of helpful practical work earning structural/transport magic and passage, then
maintenance, nursery/kinship duties or negotiated network access governing advancement.
Distinguish acquisition, first use and later work; do not erase a changed acquisition because
an unrelated later obligation appears. Record discarded mechanics and retained departures.

Two local claims have separate rules:

1. Acquisition transfer is supported by this test only if at least two direct plans introduce
   service for another inhabitant as the first-power qualification, all three staged plans
   preserve their supplied qualification without adding that service prerequisite, and the
   assigned staged chapter actually depicts the preserved acquisition and first use without
   restoring that prerequisite. A contrary staged plan or depicted chapter event kills the
   consistent acquisition-transfer claim. Missing coverage or missing controls is inconclusive.
2. Broad repetition avoidance is a lead only if at least two direct plans reproduce the target
   engine, all three staged plans and the staged chapter avoid it, preserve their source rules,
   and do not replace it with one repeated calibration-and-support engine. Any staged
   recurrence or shared replacement engine kills this consistent-avoidance claim. Invalid
   outputs, failed controls or missing chapter coverage prevent a positive conclusion.

These are procedural feasibility rules and located model-reading observations, not a
creativity score, independent reader validation or production qualification. Record pleasure,
friction, voice, explanatory load and desire to read on as unblinded exploratory observations
only. Do not rank candidates or use those observations to revise these inputs or select a book.
Even a narrow positive result does not license a production default here. No conditional
