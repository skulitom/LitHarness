# Concrete world seeds: local control, persistent plot repetition

The richer world seed changes concrete actions, but does not solve the recurring story
pattern. Both targeted v1 baselines again introduce drowned orchards. Their v2 counterparts
use the supplied pumice habitats and distinct inhabitants, yet return to irrigation,
displaced communities, controlled passages and support powers. The fresh pair provides a
counterexample to promoting v2: the v1 treatment keeps later growth centered on weapon
invention, while v2 moves it toward a bridge and evacuation.

Fresh seeding remains enabled by default using `invention-seed.v1`. The new v2 policy remains
available explicitly with `--seed-version invention-seed.v2`. Neither version guarantees
originality. This experiment does not establish distance from any training distribution.

The new habitat and inhabitant properties are consequential in the initial successes, not
merely descriptive insertions. The mirror case uses charge-sensing inhabitants and fracture
attraction; the glacier case uses traded memories, pores and attracting grit; the shell case
uses acoustic deformation and echo navigation. Each also describes a later interaction with
the conditions. That local control coexists with the broader repetition.

The recurrence is more specific than the presence of water. v2 case 0 adds a rainy destination
and officials controlling displaced families. Case 1 develops needle orchards, irrigation
guilds and refugee access even with a pumice seed. Case 2 was intentionally aquatic, but its
later magical payoff becomes a bridge and evacuation instead of continuing the baseline's
weapon progression. This is one paired counterexample, not a general quality ranking.

Names also recur: the glacier baseline again uses Mara Venn, and v2 retains Mara. Both fresh
treatments use Rusk despite neither seed supplying a name. Vey and Sere variants remain in
the batch. Distinct response hashes do not imply distinct story families.

## Development and final implementation

The registered `development-1` preserves its discovery and seed exactly, with no reserved
machinery names. Its independently generated fields explicitly develop cooperative
crossings, reconnaissance and evacuation. The middle of its arc retains the irrigation
diversion investigation; its high-level magic emphasizes linked shadow structures. This
confirms continuation of the recurring material in this artifact, not just a search hit in
the initial setting paragraph. It still requires precision editing. No chapters were
generated and no plan was accepted into a book.

v2 remains an opt-in input experiment. The code retains explicit version replay, and its v1
regression hash matches the prior stored brief. Default CLI calls still allocate fresh labels;
author precedence, retries and provenance boundaries remain intact. A regression test checks
that ordinary concepts use v1 and that both versions replay through the real CLI stages.

The result narrows what this approach accomplished: additional physical inputs can govern
local invention while familiar plot purposes and high-level powers persist. It does not
resolve the generator's concentration, establish book-length variety or license an automatic
novelty gate. These are two targeted regression probes, one new draw and one development,
with no independent reader signal or candidate selection.
