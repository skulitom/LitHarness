## B. Progressive disclosure, with a matched installment control

Conjecture: knowing later events before writing may encourage advance interpretation or tidy
explanations. Both conditions write the same three installments and receive their own complete
earlier prose verbatim (apart from boundary whitespace). Both use high reasoning, fixed before
arm A runs; arm A does not select B's writer or settings. There is no original reference prose.

The source is the same original chapter's protected record, divided at source-defined points:
F01-F06 through recognition of zero payment; F07-F14 through the first private award; F15-F30
through the final status. The existing scene separator follows installment two. These research
boundaries are not production rules or a demand that all books use this narrative.

The ahead condition sees later source installments from its first request. The staged
condition sees only the current and earlier source installments. Both receive the same current
assignment, current literal sequence and accumulated prose, and must stop at the same assigned
event without wrapping up or previewing. Earlier text is never rewritten. At installment three
there is no future block in either input; differences can persist through their different
earlier outputs. This is three-part disclosure, not token-by-token ignorance of future events
within the current installment. Both share knowledge of the writing task and its boundaries.

Pre-call source review removes F01's provenance-only reference to a later lookup and torn page,
and clarifies in F06 that the sit/wait instruction was the earlier briefing, before the light
change. All other fact descriptions remain identical. This addresses the preceding arm's
located source ambiguity equally in both cells. Both use scoped source notes: no future coat,
purchase, total, rule interpretation or rank information is supplied early through global
notes. Literal occurrences are partitioned 2/1/7, preserving all ten across the chapter.
The exact chapter, original map, adjusted source, partition and amendment are hashed locally.

Run complete chains in order ahead-1, staged-1, staged-2, ahead-2, three sequential calls each.
Stored names use full/focused as in A. Twelve calls, four assembled complete chapters. Preserve
every installment and every literal mismatch; no redraws or repairs. Inspect both installments
and complete chapters. Record source-future assertions before release, unnecessary previews,
forced closing reflections, repeated explanation and boundary repetition with paragraph
locations. Distinguish narrator foreknowledge from a justified character inference, source
information from new invention, and required current interpretation from extra restatement.
These are located readings, not an automatic anticipation or redundancy metric.
