benefit from withholding the goal. Both broad claims remain **OBSERVED**, without a positive
feasibility conclusion. Do not force partial late recurrence into an identical first-power

## What the controls allow us to conclude

The direct controls reproduce the earlier engine on all three goals. The separate mechanics
objects constrain the staged openings: their specified initial exercises and usable effects
survive instead of being replaced by those direct controls' newly invented service trials.
The original desires and sibling disagreements also remain. This is an observed local
result for a procedure adding another call, explicit rules and a longer brief. It does not
isolate which ingredient causes the change, and the narrow gate-shift claim is **OBSERVED**,
not a retrospectively registered literary success.

The consistent-staging rule demanded more than first-acquisition retention. Across the goals,
the six designs use assessed technical practice; two blind plans use the same thermal/wax
release procedure. Familiar repair sites and access disputes return in later staged growth,
especially blind-1 and linked-3, and blind-2 adds another service condition. These partial
recurrences should not be relabelled as identical emergency-repair acquisitions, but neither
do they demonstrate the clean, varied continuation required by the rule. The registered
non-convergence/complete-avoidance requirement is unmet. This is why the broader staging
claim stays OBSERVED rather than being promoted from one improved transition.

The withholding rule additionally required a contrast against recurring linked controls.
The linked plans already preserve their specified acquisition and offer departures too;
their later additions do not supply the clean claimed separation from the blind plans.
Blind-2 and blind-3 also share the heat/wax family. There is no supported withholding-specific
benefit. Do not call different temperatures, renamed systems or the absence of a nursery
proof of out-of-distribution invention.

All three goals concern preserving a family object or service. That source choice limits
any inference about varied ambitions, combat, exploration or fresh empty-brief invention.
There is one draw per condition and no prose-stage control. A further test would need fresh
goals and the normal subsequent stages before recommending a new default. The current
result supplies a specific transition to test, not a validated solution or a reader verdict.
