# Let a source-derived attention trace govern narration

Registered 2026-09-07 before any call, following the operator's acceptance of investigating
how narration chooses and handles attention. The preceding personal interpretation remains
a conjecture. Repeated explanations in the constraint diagnostic do not establish their cause.
This experiment tests the use of a represented attention trajectory, with matched exposure to
that representation. It adds no production agent or quality evaluator.

One source-only Codex invocation proposes an attention trace for six fixed contiguous intervals:

Compatible conjectures about immediate concern remain explicitly provisional, not new canon.
Reference-ID validation cannot prove that the natural-language fields respect knowledge timing.

Four fresh, independent full chapters receive identical source, required narration, trace,
system and 1500-1800-word aim. Only rendering_mode changes:

1. Background: use the trace as available character understanding while making ordinary choices
   of narrative focus, emphasis, duration and transitions.
2. Operative: use its foreground/peripheral distinctions and interruptions to govern those
   choices. Realize the allocation in the narration; the trace is not required exposition.

Order: background-1, operative-1, operative-2, background-2. Both draws are retained. There is
no trace-free fresh baseline: this does not estimate the effect of adding a trace at all. The
contrast is the explicit direction to use an identical trace. A background writer may use it
spontaneously, and an operative writer may merely explain it; either limits discrimination.
Two writer draws share one trace, so they are not independent replications of trace derivation.

## Distinction from earlier attempts

Earlier action plans specified attempts/outcomes; optional-narration work changed which facts
had to be explicit; source withholding changed what the writer knew; paragraph revision asked
for continuity of attention without representing its changing target. Here world/event coverage
and attention information are matched, and the use of a source-anchored trajectory changes.
This is still a prompt-mediated intervention and may fail like the others. No claim that an
attention record is a validated cognitive mechanism follows from implementing one.

## Readings fixed before outputs

Read the trace and all four complete chapters. Audit every original unit against each chapter,
including optional omissions, required losses, changes in timing, invented details and literal
formatting. For each of the six intervals, locate where foreground material receives attention,
what happens to peripheral material, whether the specified pressure changes the focus, and
whether the unresolved concern remains open or is immediately interpreted into a conclusion.
Record absent, contrary and uncertain realization, not just confirming passages.

In particular, distinguish attention enacted through perception/action/duration from a sentence
that merely announces the character's concern. Locate repeated explanations after their apparent
basis; retain useful causal explanation and interiority as separate observations. Inspect opening,
middle and ending. Do not count explanation words as a quality metric or require every sentence
to pass a new craft rule. More mentions of a foreground object do not establish better attention.

If both conditions dwell similarly, the contrast was weak or unrealized. If operative narration
only adds concern summaries, record that failure. If apparent reduction comes from losing facts,
events or required understanding, record the tradeoff. A delivered allocation change is not a
quality improvement. Agent readings are defect harvest, not labels, rankings or selection.
Report OBSERVED only; no causal estimate, winner, production policy or new reader mechanism.
