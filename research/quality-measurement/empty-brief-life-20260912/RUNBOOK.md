# Empty-brief discovery: remove the conditional life instruction

The operator noticed that the fresh chapter again uses Mara, water and permission-based
conflict. Its actual first discovery request contains none of those story terms and no
previous proposal. The first model response invents them. The previous life-scope test
changed expansion of two supplied stories; it did not establish diversity on an empty brief.

CONJECTURE: the conditional unfamiliar-life sentence can still elicit nonhuman family and
care/access dependencies in empty-brief discovery. Test its complete removal against fresh
unchanged controls before proposing another default. This does not presume that the sentence
is the only cause, that water is intrinsically bad, or that its removal ensures originality.

## Six frozen calls

Use the baseline chapter's original 2048-bit Base64 seed as seed 1, and generate two further
independent 2048-bit random integers through the existing default Base64 renderer before
dispatch. Each seed receives the unchanged production discovery request and the same request
with only the exact conditional sentence removed. Keep the author brief empty. Order:
control-1, omit-1, omit-2, control-2, control-3, omit-3. No names, prohibited settings,
previous stories, generated examples, critiques or comparator-book material enter either arm.
No world-seed palette or native sampler change is introduced.

Freeze all six requests, seeds, source, protocol, runner, reused dispatch implementation and
native binary; commit after one-worker handoff and before calls. Use gpt-6-astra at medium
effort through subscription Codex, 2400 output tokens, 600-second timeout and the ordinary
discovery schema. Hold the root task's existing shared-box lock. Six calls, 150000 recorded
tokens, sequentially; one in-flight call can overrun the token stop. No retries, fallback,
implicit resume, API key, live-provider test flag or reset. The reused dispatcher retains
all attempts, responses, native traces, usage and failures and stops on unknown usage or
frozen-file drift. Validate every answer with Discovery.from_invention; keep refusals.

## Reading and limits

Read all three fields of all six first outputs in order. Locate the protagonist, setting,
initiating pursuit, power and central conflict. Separately record whether nonhuman kinship
creates a new nursery/care/access obligation, and whether repair, shared water and permission
bargaining remain the main engine. A changed name or an omitted nursery alone does not
resolve the broader repeated template. These are unblinded located descriptions, not an
originality score, reader label, candidate ranking or a training-distribution measurement.

The omission supports a local contribution claim only if at least two controls produce the
kinship dependency, no omission does, and all omissions remain valid story proposals with
a usable opening and progression. Otherwise the contrast is inconclusive or killed by an
omission failure. Any continued repair/access template in an omission kills the broader
claim that this change solves the operator's recurrence problem. Report that separately.
Three seeds are input replicates on one task; they do not establish independent audience
responses or generalization across models. Prompt length and meaning are not isolated.

This experiment authorizes no automatic reroll, ranking, promotion or production feedback.
Do not change the default as a repetition fix if the broader claim fails. Preserve the
earlier two-source result and the new transfer counterexample together. Further work needs
its own concrete lead. Keep raw prose under ignored runs and commit derived evidence only.
