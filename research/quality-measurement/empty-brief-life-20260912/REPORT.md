repetition problem**. Every assigned output still centers water-based craft or repair as
the route to power and access. Five use Mara as the protagonist; the other uses Nell.
Removing the sentence avoids the added nonhuman kinship strand in these three draws,
but changes neither the dominant activity nor the recurring access constraints.

No discovery default is changed on the strength of this experiment. The narrower continuity
instruction tested separately does not address invention diversity.

## Claims and limits

The registered narrow contribution rule is met even without treating the third control's
care strand as a compulsory bargain: the first two controls add the specified kinship
dependency, all omissions avoid it, and every omission retains a valid opening and
progression proposal. This supports a local contribution of the sentence to that strand.
It does not identify the sentence as the only possible source of such material.

The broader repetition claim is **REFUTED under its registered rule**: all three omissions
retain repair, shared water and permission, ownership or contract constraints as central
story material. The treatment cannot be presented as making fresh invention reliably diverse.
The unchanged controls also differ in surface material despite replayed inputs; a single
cleaner or renamed output would have been insufficient evidence.

These are unblinded located readings of one model and task, across three seed inputs, one
of them previously observed. They are not independent audience judgments, a quality score,
or evidence about being outside a training distribution. Prompt length and meaning change
together. The result locates the recurring pattern in the first invention response; it
does not isolate which remaining instruction, interaction or model tendency produces it.

The earlier two-supplied-source life-scope finding remains local evidence, with this new
empty-brief result beside it. The next investigation must distinguish causes of the broader
repair/access pattern; removing the family subplot or renaming Mara does not establish that.
