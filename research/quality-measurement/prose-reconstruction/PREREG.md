## Meaning-first reconstruction

Use two substrates fixed before drafting: the existing criticized complete Chapter 1, and
literal/no-target replicate 1. For each, one model call extracts a literal fact ledger covering
every source paragraph, including thoughts, motives, uncertainty, knowledge claims and printed
lines. Metaphors become the literal information they express. No story repair is licensed.
Inspect both ledgers against their complete sources before reconstruction. Corrections follow
the same recorded, source-only, pre-dependent-call rule as context conversion.

One reconstruction call per substrate receives only its fact ledger and verbatim printed lines.
It does not receive the original prose, a preferred rewrite or critique. It may regroup
paragraphs but must preserve the complete event sequence, meaning and display sequence.
These are whole-substrate reconstructions, extending the earlier six-paragraph edit test.
Check numeric tokens and exact display sequence; read each full result against its own source
for changed meaning and the located habits. A structural pass does not authorize publication.
