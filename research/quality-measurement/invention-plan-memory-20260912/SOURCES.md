# Source and local inference

The earlier [history protocol](../invention-memory-20260911/RUNBOOK.md) used short premises.
The [latest complete inventory](../invention-verbalized-20260911/REPORT.md) identifies repeated
material in expanded plans. These artifacts motivate checking history coverage; they do not
establish that adding full plans will prevent the repetition. Registration freezes the
underlying source receipts. Only their original generated prose, never report text, enters
this isolated reference context.

[Dynamic Context Evolution v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07147v1), section 3.3, describes
conditioning later generations on previous ideas. Its broader method also uses changing
strategies and rejection mechanisms. This pilot adopts none of its candidate filters,
probability claims or embedding-based quality/diversity interpretations and is not a
replication. The paper does not test our short-premise versus complete-plan comparison.
The versioned snapshot was rechecked on 2026-09-12; its hash is in sources.json. No paper
example or other third-party prose enters a request.
