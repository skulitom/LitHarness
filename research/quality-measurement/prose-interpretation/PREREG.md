# Prescribed interpretation ablation, 2026-09-06

CONJECTURE, not an established mechanism: repeated interpretive conclusions in the source
and causal plan may encourage the writer to restate a lesson even after the staged actions
have changed. The preceding event-order trial located this mismatch in both early-step
drafts. This experiment removes that prescription while holding their staged actions fixed.

## Frozen intervention

Use the preceding trial's reviewed earlier-step request, before any draft output, as control.
No new planner, candidate selector, critic or prose editor is called. Prepare the treatment
with occurrence-checked, source-reviewed replacements under ignored runs. Preserve all twelve
action lines byte-for-byte, their order, event times, starting status, prices, eligibility,
payments, visibility and reveal boundaries, and common ending. Retain background and career
material unchanged as a contrast with the specifically targeted interpretation.

Replace the repeated assertion that better reading entails later action with the conditional
mechanical cost of time spent reading without a priced attempt. Keep intentions and observable
responses, removing or reducing consequence fields that supply interpretive verdicts. Preserve
their physical, accounting and knowledge constraints. Remove the compulsory retrospective
lesson about passive compliance; allow inference and interiority without requiring a verbal
conclusion. This intentionally relaxes a planned mental outcome, not a physical world rule.
The source review must distinguish these changes from factual corrections and disclose any
remaining interpretation in the unchanged context. Do not claim total removal of thematic input.

The treatment is a bundle: conditional framing, reduced causal commentary and relaxed mental
endpoint. It cannot identify their separate contributions. It differs from prior literalization
and reconstruction arms by removing the requirement to express an interpretation, rather than
rewriting its wording. It differs from focused context by retaining all other source material.
No corpus text, examples, preferred prose, earlier drafts or reader judgments enter either arm.

## Execution and stopping

Commit this registration and RUNBOOK before any model call. Four fresh calls only, in order
full-1, focused-1, focused-2, full-2; two per condition. Cache names do not imply a quality rank.
Use the tested isolated Codex CLI with requested gpt-6-astra/high, ChatGPT subscription login,
separate hashed system files, empty working directories and tools disabled. No direct API,
API credentials, reset, retry, redraw, fallback or automatic model selection. Retain failures.
Resolved model and dollar cost remain unknown unless independently present in the envelope.

Stop dispatch at 90000 cumulative input + output + separately reported reasoning tokens;
cached input is already included. This is a dispatch stop, not a hard bound on final output.
Missing usage, unrecognized notices, actual tool calls, changed frozen input, source conflict,
or any invocation failure stops the run. Inspect processes and acquire the shared box lock;
one model arm at a time, with no heavy check beside generation.

## Reading and scope

Read all four whole outputs in generation order. Locate action/payment delivery, end-of-scene
retrospective claims relative to actual prior behavior, repeated explanation of the same result,
background inventory, source violations and losses of causal intelligibility or character
motivation. Document retained problems as well as apparent omissions; absence of one quoted
phrase alone does not establish improvement. The unchanged background is a descriptive
contrast, not a statistically qualified negative control or a new metric.

Agent reading is defect harvest, never a quality label, ranking, efficacy estimate, production
gate or qualification. No draft is selected. One repeatedly studied premise and two draws per
condition cannot establish reliability or transfer. Original source, canon and production
drafting remain untouched. Commit code, derived numbers, identifiers and bounded observations;
all generated prose and narrative-specific amendments stay ignored.
