Four fresh opening scenes were generated and read in full. Reducing prescribed interpretation
did not remove the located background inventories, repeated scheme explanations or unpaid-action
recaps. All four delivered the assigned early payment before withdrawal. No literary efficacy,
quality ranking, reliability or production qualification is established.

## What to investigate next

The external literature in RESEARCH.md distinguishes planning/coherence, realization and
analytical theme extraction. It does not establish a cure for this repository's prose.
The present ablation leaves a long shared context and an action sequence dominated by checking,
stopping and watching. It cannot distinguish their contribution from the writer's defaults.

A useful next diagnostic would expose the writer to a genuinely small, scene-only brief on
a fresh premise before rebuilding another planning layer. Prior focused-context trials still
carried substantial mandatory context; this would test a different input boundary. It should
retain only the current situation, concrete objective, physical rules and knowledge limits,
without a compulsory list of interpretations. Such a probe can locate defects and test what
the transport produces under a simpler brief; it cannot establish a general quality ceiling,
license automatic context selection or prove that fewer tokens mean better prose.
