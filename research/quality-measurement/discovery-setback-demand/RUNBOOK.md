Frozen reading order: keep-2, omit-1, keep-1, omit-2. This is source-unexposed inspection,
not blinded or independent judging. Then read all treatments in generation order and
trace located additions between premise, treatment and chapter.

Record paragraph locations for permission/payment activity, institutional actors,
power retention explained through dispossession, incidental precision, explanation of
speech/action and motives, magical action, usable progression and premise displacement.
Separate inherited details from treatment additions and new drafting additions. Include
counterexamples; work and payments alone are not evidence of an administrative plot.
Do not score, rank or select outputs, infer quality from word counts, or ask the operator
to supply labels. Inspection is not a qualified reader mechanism.

Persistence of administrative additions in both omit trajectories defeats the narrow
expectation that removing this demand is sufficient on this source. Their disappearance
would warrant transfer testing, not establish a general solution. Mixed or missing
trajectories remain inconclusive. Report treatment and prose results separately: removal
from a treatment does not imply removal from its draft. Four trajectories, one selected
prior premise, stochastic treatment differences, the shorter prompt and simplified
drafting path cannot establish repeatability, causal mediation or production quality.
