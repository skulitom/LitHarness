# Setbacks-demand deletion: no demonstrated opening-quality fix

All four treatments and four opening drafts completed. Both modified treatments omit
the explicit retained-through-setbacks inventory that appears in their controls, but
both still invent licensing institutions and access conflicts. Their chapters begin
with those conflicts. Removing the demand was not sufficient on this source; this
does not establish that the demand has no effect or that either condition is better.

## Located result

Paragraphs are one-based within each treatment field or complete opening. All openings
were read in registered order before any fresh treatment was inspected.

| Trajectory | Treatment locations | Opening locations and transfer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| keep-1 | world P3, opening P1, growth P3 | P7-10 retain licensed copying rights and permission from a supervisor. The growth field explicitly answers the retained-capability question. |
| omit-1 | world P1, opening P1, growth P2-3 | P1-12 turn the prescribed bribe into the opening interaction. Licensing and legal/economic uses of magic persist without a separate retention inventory. |
| omit-2 | world P2, opening P1/P4, growth P3 | P1-8 begin at the prescribed cordon. P24 carries forward the treatment's explanation of action through character identity. |
| keep-2 | world P1, opening P2, growth P4 | P17-18 retain tithe-men, ropes and restricted access. The growth field explicitly lists dispossession while power remains. |

All four drafts include magical discovery and explicit personal progression feedback.
Successful deliberate magical action appears in both conditions. These are located
features, not evidence that the manipulation improves reader appeal. Speech-manner and
motive explanations also remain in both conditions. Some are already present in the
treatment; others are added while drafting. Exact incidental quantities are both
inherited and newly invented. This experiment did not manipulate the quantity policy.
Omit-2 growth P2 also makes learned words consumable, which needs checking against the
operator's retained-progression direction; this sample cannot attribute that choice
to the deletion.

## Secondary source-to-draft observations

The local inspection retains exact quotations and verifies their locations in code.
That establishes the quoted text and its provenance, not a literary-quality measure.

- **A recurrence changes during drafting:** omit-1 world P1 retains the common premise's
  every-ninth-night cycle; opening P5 changes it to every night through a nine-out-of-nine
  formulation. This is separate from the administrative-content hypothesis.
- **A planned discovery sequence is not completed:** keep-2 treatment opening P4-6
  intends further listenings, entering the library, hearing a book and escaping with it.
  Its chapter ends at P36, selecting the next object after the first listening. The
  draft also adds a fourth tin object absent from the treatment's named inventory.
  The treatment's later fourth-listening wording is ambiguous; the cause of the added
  object or early ending is not established.
- **An object-role ambiguity is made sharper:** omit-2 treatment opening P2-3 calls an
  item a lamp-key and uses it in a door. Draft P15 defines it as a wick-adjusting tool,
  while P17 keeps the door action. The inconsistency is not wholly invented downstream.
- **Explanatory wording crosses the stage boundary:** omit-2 treatment opening P4 and
  chapter P24 explain an attempt through character identity; keep-1 treatment opening
  P3 and chapter P30 share a child-teaching comparison. Omit-2 chapter P14 separately
  adds a psychological explanation to a simple planned kneeling action.

The last findings leave distinct problems to investigate: the content invention
chooses, the rhetorical wording it hands to drafting, and what drafting changes or
fails to realize. This test does not identify a single root cause or validate a remedy
for any of them. Do not promote another prohibition or an automatic quality gate.

## Execution and limits

Registration predates all calls. The full schedule passed the offline conservative
budget projection, and no quota refusal, transport stop, retry, redraw or selection
occurred. All treatments passed the shared production invention validator. Source,
request, transport and usage controls pass; the comparison contains every paragraph
of every retained opening and treatment. The execution record owns usage, checks and
artifact hashes. Raw CLI internal-turn counts are distinguished from completion calls.

This is one previously generated port-and-salvage premise, two trajectories per
condition, and an isolated drafting task. Both conditions already omit the previous
institutional comparison. There is no full production world/context/acceptance loop,
blinded quality judgment, model ranking or production adoption. No accepted book or
production prompt changed.
