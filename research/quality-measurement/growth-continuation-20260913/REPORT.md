production loop. Wren bought Load once and Duration once; their typed holdings
persisted, and the next writer received the earlier Load purchase. No capability
received a second paid investment in this live run. That boundary is covered by the
scripted integration test, not established by these chapters. This is an engineering
observation, with partial status coverage and the audit corrections below; it does
not qualify a reader mechanism or establish literary quality.
