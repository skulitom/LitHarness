## Operative rules and the inherited gap

Mara's chapter shows one wrong assessment answer, followed by inspection and a corrected
selection before the progress counter accepts that joint (P36-40). Later completion therefore
has an explicit path from failure to qualification. Casting includes cleaning, contacting
surfaces and exhaling while tracing (P50-54). The two seam lengths are below ten centimetres,
and the two five-mana expenditures reduce twenty to fifteen to ten (P49, P54, P78).

The first fitting fails at a displayed 68 N despite the intact seam (P58-67). The distinction
between bond strength and surrounding rotten wood directly changes her next action. She
checks that permanent machinery holds the gate and unloads the first seam before dismissing
it (P68-77), then uses a sound metal replacement. Load approaches but does not exceed 100 N;
pausing lets it ease, and the permanent catch engages with twelve seconds remaining (P80-95).
The magic does not supply motion or become a permanent support. Those rules matter to the
depicted outcome rather than appearing only as background numbers.

Wren's chapter reproduces the source's fifth and eleventh incorrect answers (P37-56). It then
states that the last nine pass and unlocks Loadstitch after the twentieth joint (P58-61).
Neither the source nor this chapter shows the two earlier predictions being successfully
reassessed. The original mechanics required correctly predicting each of twenty failure
points; the chapter's own assessment message repeats the task for each assembly (P24).
This is an unresolved inherited qualification gap, not a defect newly invented solely in prose.

Wren's 600 N body weight versus the 100 N allowance kills the handhold proposal before she
casts (P63-70). Direct contact and dryness govern whether a seam is accepted (P72-78). A valid
eight-centimetre seam spends five mana, displays 14 N before she steps on it, and reaches
62 N during crossing (P78-83). Her own crossing takes nine seconds; subsequent coaxing and
hesitation supply elapsed time before the creature crosses with four seconds left (P84-94).

The source plan's compressed timing is thus given intervening action. The award reports sixty
seconds of measured load (P96); the chapter supplies a nonzero slab load before Wren steps on
it, rather than claiming her body stayed on it for sixty seconds. These details address two
ambiguities in the earlier plan, but are not an independent engineering certification. The
first seam expires before her return uses another (P94, P100), so no simultaneous-seam violation
is shown. Living tissue, liquids as joined material and the later extensions are not otherwise
tested by the action; their absence cannot establish that all powers were handled correctly.

## Reading observations and next decision

Several choices have legible consequences in the prose. Mara's rushed inspection causes a
specific failure after she has already demonstrated she can inspect more carefully (P64-67).
The mother's first reply changes the immediate uncertainty from whether she is alive to how
to reach her (P102-110). Wren's familiar-material assumption fails visibly (P39-44); thirst
and frustration disrupt the expertise she normally relies on (P48-57). Her conversation with
the creature supplies small relational actions beyond receiving instructions (P85-99).

Those are located observations, not scores or evidence that the intended readership would
enjoy either chapter. Large portions of both openings still concern tests, forces and tool
use, and the inherited assessment issue is unresolved. Neither chapter establishes a novel's
range of characterization, escalation or sustained enjoyment.

The useful result is that the current writer can preserve the different supplied pursuit in
this pair. There is no observed collapse back into the family-rescue/return-home pattern for
Wren. This gives a reason to focus the next fresh-start diversity experiment on invention and
planning, while tracking whether their first outputs already repeat before prose begins.
It does not justify silently switching provider defaults, selecting the more appealing chapter,
or declaring the repetition problem solved. The single known parent pair, differing source
lengths (796 versus 2002 words), one draw each and changed CLI limit generalization.
