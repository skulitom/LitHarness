## What remains uncertain

This was a small duplication-removal package: frequency, placement and length change
together. It does not test all repeated facts, remove every explanatory duty, isolate a
model effect or test a new narrative agent. Two draws of one source cannot establish a
general absence of effect. The narrower conclusion is that the located problem survived
this particular deletion in both retained treatment outputs.

The original input has unresolved authority tensions. A hidden-truth entry forbids
rendering the scheme-visibility event that the scene plan explicitly requires. The plan
also pays a standing invigilator for a seat-related action. Timing descriptions and the
restriction on exact non-system numbers require care when interpreting compliance. These
are shared input properties, not causes established by this comparison. The two corrected
knowledge headings in a later source variant were not tested here.

No production prompt or manuscript was changed. A subsequent diagnostic should distinguish
what a scene must enact, what a character knows and what the narrator is forbidden to
disclose, while retaining required story facts. That is a proposed question, not a qualified
mechanism or a promised fix. The present evidence does not justify another writing agent.

The current code audit narrows that proposal. Context rendering still applies the blanket
hidden-truth embargo (`domain/context.py`, hidden-section rendering). An existing exclusion
mechanism in `domain/worlds.py` uses explicit reader-disclosure state records: no-position
records or comparable records at/before the scene release a claim; missing, future and
incomparable positions do not. `application/planner.py` supplies the scene order key but
appends the scene-plan prose independently of that classification. A matching disclosure
record can already prevent this collision; a prose requirement alone cannot. Thus the
next question concerns reconciliation with the existing disclosure mechanism, not building
a second knowledge system or treating every current-scene instruction as permission to
disclose a secret. This static inspection was at the registration commit, not a fresh
production reconstruction or evidence that this collision caused the observed style.
