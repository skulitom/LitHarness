# Premise before development: generation pilot

## Question and boundary

The operator authorized testing the next proposed fix on 2026-09-08. The conjecture is
that making a public premise before detailed invention gives later stages a clearer
pursuit and progression promise to preserve. This pilot generates examples for located
defect inspection; it is not a reader-quality measure, model ranking or production gate.

The preceding fixed-concept experiments are recorded under
`runs/diagnostics/one-clean-want-overview-20260908/`. They leave open whether a listing
can repair a weak underlying pursuit without inventing unsupported relationships.
Their critiques and generated story material must not enter this pilot's prompts.

## Frozen procedure

Use a blank author brief, third person, 24 first-arc scenes, no writer dossier and no
exemplar text. Use the same configured Claude Code model in every call. Retain each
first response, including failures; no redraw, preference selection or editing after
reading. The run root is `runs/diagnostics/premise-before-development-20260908/`.

Run these arms in the order shown:

1. **Current order:** the production discovery request, concept development from that
   treatment, the current scoped precision pass when its scheduling check fires,
   then the production supplied-concept public-listing request.
2. **Premise first:** invent a public premise from the same blank author brief and the
   standing LitRPG discovery direction, before requesting statistics or a treatment.
   Supply that first response as a story promise to the production discovery and
   concept-development requests. Apply the same scoped precision pass, then use the
   same final public-listing request as the control, with only the developed concept
   as source. Do not give the final listing writer the initial premise separately.

The promise wrapper asks development to preserve the person, pursuit, magical
possibility and stakes of that premise, and leaves unpromised particulars open.
The initial premise task asks for an understandable situation, desire, magical
possibility and personal progression in about a hundred words. It supplies no
setting, character, plot, exact quantity, special power, example blurb or story text.
Freeze the exact task and wrapper in the runner before dispatch.

The precision stage uses the production parser and edit application. Preserve both
the raw developed concept and the prepared concept. Do not quietly discard a failed
edit or replace it manually. A structural failure ends that arm; the other arm may
still run. A transport/authentication/quota failure stops all remaining calls.

## Read before interpreting

Read each final listing without its source first, then inspect it against its own
discovery and developed concept. For the premise-first arm, also compare the initial
promise with the development. Record exact artifact locations for:

- a sentence whose referent, situation or pursuit needs unstated source knowledge;
- a magical object conflated with a personal ability;
- a causal dependency or motive introduced by compression rather than its source;
- a premise promise displaced or contradicted by later development;
- incidental precision in ordinary narration, distinguished from system values;
- a progression path expressed as usable personal capability or merely as an award.

These are inspection questions, not scored features, a quality bar or automated
acceptance criteria. Record uncertainties and counterexamples. Do not ask a model to
rate, prefer, select or certify either arm. Do not send this inspection protocol to
any generation call. No accepted book changes and no production promotion follow
automatically from the result.

## Confounds and limits fixed in advance

The stories are invented independently from the same brief. Content, random sampling,
call order, an extra call and promise-preservation framing differ between arms. This
is a comparison of generation procedures, not an isolated causal test of ordering.
A readable output cannot establish repeatability, reader appeal or Chapter 1 quality.
A defective output is retained and locates a failure in that trajectory only.
There is no chapter draft in this pilot: first inspect whether the premise survives
development before paying for world construction and chapter generation.
