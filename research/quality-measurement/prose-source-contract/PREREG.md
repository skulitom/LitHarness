content should cease becoming character behavior or extra fidelity commentary while the
guarded story meaning survives. Persistence at the same locations weakens that conjecture;
guard violations, missing meaning or displaced equivalent explanation do not support it.

their required first meanings. If it merely summarizes the whole chapter, loses embodied

the proposed repair. If the free system produces comparable length and the same additions,
