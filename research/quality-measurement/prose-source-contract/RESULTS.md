## Deeper diagnosis and its limits

The conjecture now worth pursuing is how much discretionary narration is spent settling
meaning that an event has already made available. That pattern survives modest shortening
and relocation of specific editorial guards. These results do not prove a model ceiling,
an inability to depict interiority, or the failure of every possible intermediary agent.
The word-target ablation is not a direct brevity instruction, and separated guards were
still supplied. This is not a clean deletion test of all editorial influence.
