# Institutional-cue deletion: insufficient in the retained samples

Three first-response treatments were generated from one fixed public premise: one
current-prompt control and two draws deleting the institutional comparison. Both
deletion draws still introduced licensing institutions, permission transactions and
explanations contrasting retained power with authority. Removing that clause alone
was not sufficient on this source. The broader priming conjecture remains unresolved;
this is neither a quality ranking nor a claim that the clause has no effect.
