# Publisher acquisition landscape: Aethon Books and Blackstone Publishing

**Compiled 2026-08-18** from web research (two parallel sourced sweeps; URLs inline).
Purpose: ground three LitHarness operator decisions — the comparator frame sentence,
comparator-corpus sourcing (preference-runbook §0–§1), and the standing question of
whether any publisher route exists for system-authored prose. Claims that could not be
verified against a primary source are flagged inline; treat flagged numbers as
historical or secondhand.

---

## 1. Aethon Books — the genre's professional selector

Aethon is the dominant professional publisher of exactly the genre LitHarness produces:
LitRPG / progression fantasy, with cultivation/xianxia as an explicitly labeled line.
Founded 2018 by Rhett C. Bruno and Steve Beaulieu; 1,500+ titles, 20M+ copies claimed,
self-reported top-10 Audible publisher, ~200 books/year, now with Simon &
Schuster–distributed retail print via the Vault partnership and a WEBTOON adaptation
pipeline (Dungeon Crawler Carl, HWFWM, Defiance of the Fall).

- Submissions: <https://aethonbooks.com/submissions/>
- 2025 review: <https://aethonbooks.com/2025/12/30/aethons-2025-in-review/>
- PW Close-Up (Vault): <https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/authors/interviews/article/98200-pw-studio-pw-close-up-vault-and-aethon-make-partnership-official-releasing-major-collaborations.html>

### What they acquire

- **Genres (current submissions page):** SFF all subgenres — named focus on hard SF,
  epic fantasy, romantasy, space opera, LitRPG, military SF, alt-history, time travel;
  thrillers (action/political/military). Cultivation lives under the LitRPG umbrella
  in the catalog, not on the submissions page.
- **Open, unagented submissions:** cover letter + first 50 pages by email; "novel-length"
  only; stand-alone or series (series favored). No response guaranteed; nudge at 90 days.
  *Flagged:* the 60k-minimum / 80k-preferred word counts and "no advances" come from a
  2019-era Authors-Publish snapshot (<https://authorspublish.com/aethon-books-now-accepting-manuscript-submissions/>),
  not the current page. Royalty rates are unpublished ("among the highest in the business"
  is their own claim).
- **The real pipeline is Royal Road:** flagship titles were proven serials signed after
  demonstrating traction, and Aethon's own book pages advertise the metrics as marketing
  copy — HWFWM "13 million views," Mark of the Fool "nearly 10 million," Runebound
  Professor "over 10 million." No published follower/view threshold exists (*flagged:*
  the "they mine RR metrics" framing traces to a partisan substack plus Aethon's own
  pages; Forbes independently notes their data-driven marketing).
- **Business model:** KU-centric ebooks (inferred from catalog, not stated policy), audio
  as co-equal product via Podium for the big series and an in-house Aethon Audio imprint,
  then format multiplication of proven IP: ebook → audio → webcomic → light novel →
  retail print → screen.

### Stated craft criteria (their fullest public statement of taste)

From the submissions page: "story, story, story" — compelling narrative, three-dimensional
characters, urgency, sufficient conflict, strong voice (first or third), good pacing,
original world-building, consistent believable characters, and **unpredictability**.
Bruno (PW, 2025): what sells is "true blockbuster content" — action-packed, escapist.
No public statement found on chapter structure, tropes rejected, or prose-level bars;
founder podcast interviews exist but transcripts were unretrievable.

### Structural profile of their successful titles

- Single-protagonist progression arc with explicit mechanics stated on the page
  (LitRPG systems, cultivation stages); subtitle taxonomy on every cover.
- Very long open-ended series (HWFWM at 13 books, Primal Hunter at 17), doorstopper
  volumes (HWFWM 1 ≈ 29 hours of audio), fast cadence.
- Blurb formula: hook premise line → tone/voice promise → genre wink → social proof
  (RR view counts) → comp titles ("perfect for fans of Cradle…").
- One star narrator per series in audio.

### AI stance — the door is closed

Three live statements: never knowingly use AI art or AI narration (Dec 2022,
<https://aethonbooks.com/2022/12/14/our-stance-on-ai-generated-art/>); AI as a writing
*aid* is "the future," but AI-written novels are explicitly not okay and **"our contracts
will begin to reflect that stance"** — i.e., anti-AI-authorship warranties (Apr 2023,
<https://aethonbooks.com/2023/04/19/our-stance-against-non-human-content/>); stance
reaffirmed in the 2025 year-in-review. The submissions page itself carries no AI clause;
the policy lives in blog posts and contracts.

## 2. Blackstone Publishing — the audio-first trade house

Founded 1987 (Ashland, OR; family-owned), audiobook house that expanded into trade
print/e-book in 2015–16. ~18,500 audiobook backlist titles, in-house studios and
printing; audio is still the biggest segment (*flagged:* staff/title counts vary by
source and date). Catalog strategy runs on big-money estate buys (Crichton, Clavell,
L'Amour), celebrity publishing (Downey Jr., R.L. Stine, Whoopi Goldberg's WhoopInk
imprint), poached midlist bestsellers, and audio-rights licensing of proven indie hits.

- About: <https://www.blackstonepublishing.com/pages/about-us>
- PW profile (2023): <https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/91861-blackstone-grows-beyond-its-audiobook-roots.html>

### What they acquire

- **Genres:** thriller/crime/mystery, military/action, SF (Hell Divers, Silo), horror,
  romance; most recent stated priorities (Black List initiative, Nov 2025): thriller,
  mystery, romance, horror, with film/TV adaptability as an explicit filter
  (<https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/99153-the-black-list-teams-with-blackstone-on-novel-contest.html>).
  Not a LitRPG house.
- **Path:** agented-only for the main list; two unagented routes exist (WhoopInk's direct
  form; the paid Black List contest). Audio rights are non-negotiable in every trade deal
  (per agent Mark Gottlieb: they have never accepted a print/ebook-only manuscript).
- **Indie relevance:** they license audio rights from proven self-published authors and
  sometimes upgrade to full trade deals — the Nicholas Sansbury Smith path (Extinction
  Cycle: ~350k self-pub copies → audio-only deal → six-book trade deal). De facto
  selection metrics are demonstrated sales, Audible chart/editorial recognition, and
  adaptation heat; no public thresholds (*flagged:* first-hand kboards reports paywalled).
- **Craft signal:** "voice and a story… a natural fit for both publication and film or TV
  adaptation" (Deneen); voice-driven narrative pitched to the audio team (*flagged:*
  phrasing from a search snippet of the June 2026 Fawns interview, not re-extracted).
  No published editor statement on dialogue density or POV-count preferences; their
  dual-narrator productions imply multi-POV comfort, but that is inference.

### AI stance

Human-narration as marketing (Blackstone Unlimited: "read only by human narrators and
not AI"); endorser of the Audio Publishers Association's AI-narration disclosure
guidelines; WhoopInk submission page states "AI works will not be considered." No
company-wide AI-manuscript policy found for the main list.

## 3. What this means for LitHarness

### 3a. Comparator corpus and frame (preference-runbook §0–§1)

1. **Aethon's catalog is the professionally-selected tier of "published-human" LitRPG**
   — the natural upper rung for the comparator frame. But the flagships (HWFWM, Primal
   Hunter, DotF) are exactly what the recognition screen will burn: genre-familiar paid
   readers will recognize them, and every recognized judgment is paid-for evidence
   thrown away.
2. **The usable sampling frame is Aethon's mid-list**: the monthly "LitRPG & Progression
   Fantasy Releases" posts (~16 titles/month, e.g.
   <https://aethonbooks.com/2026/07/15/august-2026-litrpg-progression-fantasy-releases/>)
   are a public, dated, professionally-vetted enumeration — a defensible frame sentence
   like *"Aethon-published LitRPG/progression fantasy, 2024–2026 monthly-release cohort,
   franchise flagships excluded"* is enumerable before paying anyone, which is what
   "the frame is the claim" requires.
3. **Two rungs, two claims.** RoyalRoad mid-list (the runbook's example frame) and
   Aethon-published are different claims: the second has passed a professional selection
   filter the first has not. Beating the Aethon mid-list is the stronger and more
   legible headline; the frame declaration should name which rung is being claimed.
4. **Covariates:** Aethon-published prose was serial-first — chapter-cadence pacing,
   explicit stats on page, single-protagonist close POV. Matching premise/length per
   `corpus-add` should also respect serial-vs-book provenance, or the covariate record
   should carry it.

### 3b. The selection function validates the instrument

Neither publisher gates on prose-quality rubrics. Both select on **revealed reader
preference**: Aethon on Royal Road view/follower traction, Blackstone on sales, Audible
charts, and adaptation heat. The market's own quality function is accumulated reader
preference — which is the construct the pairwise engine (§69) purchases directly. This
is external corroboration for the refoundation's choice of instrument, and further
evidence against the refuted proxy metrics (BRIEF §2): the industry doesn't use
prose-feature gates either.

### 3c. Craft-criteria coverage check

Aethon's stated taste, mapped to current instrumentation:

| Publisher criterion | LitHarness coverage |
|---|---|
| Urgency / conflict / hooks | Promise/payoff ledger + scene-delta (§71) — closest existing instrument |
| Three-dimensional characters | Reader-named defect "no interiority" (2026-08-18 read) — named, not yet instrumented |
| Voice | Reader-named defects file; no dedicated instrument |
| Pacing | Scene-delta partially; serial chapter-cadence pacing unmeasured |
| **Unpredictability** | **No instrument names it** — candidate annotation dimension |
| Consistent believable characters | Integrity gates cover consistency of *fact*, not of characterization |

### 3d. The publisher route is closed, and that is clarifying

Aethon puts anti-AI-authorship warranties in contracts; Blackstone's one open-submission
imprint bans AI works and the house markets human-only narration. **No pairwise win rate
changes this — the exclusion is provenance-based, not quality-based.** Consequences:

- Publisher acceptance can never serve as a validity check or a goal for this system;
  the pre-registered pairwise bar is not just the chosen instrument, it is the *only*
  external door. The §61 refoundation (cutting publication cadence as a goal) is
  confirmed from the market side.
- If the gated RL-environment side quest or any commercial angle ever revives, the only
  channel is direct (KDP) — with Amazon's own AI-disclosure regime, which this research
  did not cover.
- Corollary for the comparator corpus: corpus excerpts come from books whose publisher
  contractually opposes AI authorship. Excerpting for private paid-reader judgment is a
  research use of purchased copies, but the operator should decide the excerpt-length
  policy deliberately (short scene-grain excerpts, purchased copies, no redistribution
  beyond reader packets).

### 3e. Structural norms as generation-side reference (not goals)

Genre-market shape, if it ever matters again: historical 60k-minimum/80k-preferred word
counts (flagged as stale), doorstopper volumes, 10+ book open series, explicit
mechanics on the page (LitHarness's state-on-page requirement is genre-aligned),
blurb formula with social proof and comps. None of this bears on the scene-grain
pairwise bar; recorded here so it doesn't have to be re-researched.
