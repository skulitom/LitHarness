# Publisher taste profile: what Aethon and Blackstone select, judged from the books

**Compiled 2026-08-18.** Companion to
[publisher-acquisition-landscape.md](publisher-acquisition-landscape.md), which covers
submission mechanics, business models, and provenance policy. This file is
**provenance-blind**: it characterizes editorial taste — themes, premise shapes,
protagonist types, tone, prose style — purely from the catalogs, blurbs, narrator
casting, and review consensus, regardless of who or what wrote the book.

Method: two parallel catalog sweeps. Aethon: 77 title-entries from five monthly release
posts (Mar 2025–Aug 2026), 16 series-starter entries, ~86 series from category pages,
14 full blurbs, review syntheses. Blackstone: 48 frontlist titles from new-release /
coming-soon collections (estate reprints excluded), 29 full blurbs, narrator-casting
data, editor statements. Frequencies are estimates from these samples, not audited
counts; low-confidence inferences flagged inline.

---

## 1. Aethon Books: the taste specification

**One line:** ordinary, rational young people who treat catastrophe as an optimization
problem, told in transparent close-third prose with snark, stat boxes, and companions,
packaged as open-ended series in self-comping lanes.

### Premise lanes (estimated share of the LitRPG line; direction 2025→2026)

| Lane | Share | Direction | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| System apocalypse / Earth-integrated | ~15–25% | holding via sequels, fewer new launches | Primal Hunter, Defiance of the Fall, Hell Difficulty Tutorial |
| Reincarnation/isekai *as frame* | ~half of new starters | steady — delivery mechanism, not the point | Runebound Professor, Dao of Money, Reborn as a Demonic Tree |
| Cultivation-with-a-twist | rising, the current lane | **rising fastest** | Dao of Money (economics), Cultivation Nerd / Science of Cultivation (science), Monarch of Profound Toxin |
| Cozy / profession / crafting | rising | **rising** | Rise of the Living Forge, Merchant Crab, Arcane Chef, Beers and Beards, Neighborhood Necromancer |
| Academy / time-loop / monster-MC / dungeon core | ~5–10% each | steady | Mark of the Fool, Die.Respawn.Repeat, The Reluctant Dungeon |
| VRMMO | ~0 in sample | **dead** (legacy catalog only) | Awaken Online (backlist) |

The acquisitions pattern inside every lane: **a familiar chassis plus exactly one
high-concept lens** ("the Dao of Money," science-vs-dao, merchant crab). Differentiation
is by twist, never by prose or structure.

### Protagonist invariant

- Male, late-teens to early-30s, ordinary-to-disadvantaged start (office worker, orphan
  with a "detrimental" talent, no-talent scientist). **Underdog-to-apex is the invariant
  arc**; the power curve must go up.
- **Competence as identity** — "highly competent and rational MC" appears verbatim as a
  selling point; the MC out-thinks the system.
- Pragmatic-good wearing a dark aesthetic (heroic necromancers, demonic tree with a
  conscience). True anti-heroes rare; villain cosplay on a good heart common.
- Voice is either humor-bearing snark (HWFWM register) or stoic-rational (DotF register).
- Loner → found family; companions and pets are a feature, not a flourish.
- Female-led essentially quarantined in the romantasy line; rare in the LitRPG line
  (Azarinth Healer the notable exception — moderate confidence).

### Emotional contract (what blurbs promise)

Effort→reward **fairness** (the grind pays), apocalypse-as-opportunity ("Where others
see doom, he sees opportunity"), escape from drudgery, competence porn, clean revenge,
found family, humor as coping. **Absent:** literary interiority, grief that isn't fuel,
tragedy, ambiguity-as-center, romance-as-center (main line), satire of the genre itself.

### Style markers

Close third, single MC, strong default. Plain transparent webserial-derived prose
("completely revised" is the stock phrase). Stat blocks standard; reviewers complain
about stat-sheet bloat and "too much telling" — **the house publishes them anyway**,
i.e., it tolerates flat interiority when progression and voice deliver. Short chapters,
cliffhanger cadence (inferred from serial origin), doorstopper volumes (20h+ audio).
Register: irreverent-but-earnest, with cozy-whimsical as a rising second register.

### Blurb template

(1) aphorism/inversion hook ("The gods chose him. He said no.") → (2) MC + ordinary-world
rupture → (3) the one twist stated plainly → (4) genre-keyword feature list → (5) social
proof (RR views) → (6) comps — which are **other Aethon books**: the list comps itself,
signaling acquisition into proven internal lanes.

### Negative space

No standalones in the main line; no prose-forward/structure-forward/ambiguity-forward
titles; no nihilism or downer arcs (dark aesthetics everywhere, tragedy nowhere);
"slow burn" appears only as a power-curve promise, never a character-study one.

## 2. Blackstone Publishing: the taste specification

**One line:** filmable, mid-length, multi-POV suspense about lying intimates and
secret-keeping families — cast for audio ensembles, structured around a bounded trap
and a final question — plus deep-series action/SF franchises for the commuter listener.

### Premise shape

**"Contained situation + concealed past"** with a literal clock or bounded space:
defending your own husband for murdering his mistress, a lockdown that ends with a body,
a murder mid-flight, a rooftop in a flood. ~60–70% of frontlist thrillers are
one-sentence pitchable; **adaptation-friendliness is explicit strategy** (the film/TV
head is also an acquiring editor; premises that read like loglines are the filter).
Balance within thrillers (estimates): domestic/psychological ~45–50%, military/action
~25–30%, procedural/PI ~20–25%. Domestic sells as standalone-then-sequelized; action/SF
is engineered for deep series (Hell Divers 12+, Tier One 8+).

### Character profile

Ordinary-person-in-extremis (domestic strand) vs professional operator (action strand),
with a middle lane of professional women in crime roles. Signature move: **mutual
deception / dual culpability** — both leads have something to hide (~half the
psych-thriller sample). Morally gray female narrators welcome; villain protagonists
rare. **Multi-POV is near-default**, verifiable through narrator casting (~60% of
sampled frontlist has 2+ audio narrators; POV count = narrator count is the tell).
Female-led ~60% of thrillers overall — near 100% domestic, near 0% military.

### Themes

Their own stated brief — love, betrayal, family, obsession — is genuinely descriptive
of the shelf, plus a fifth unstated pillar: **survival**. Recurring: betrayal within
intimacy (strongest single pattern), family secrets surfacing, someone-is-watching
obsession, small towns with secrets.

### Style markers

Twist-forward, reveal-structured; blurbs end on a question; endorsements sell the
ending. Mid-length propulsion: 9–12 hour runtimes (~300–400 pages) cluster tightly —
almost nothing doorstop-sized. Audio-first craft: bounded-clock premises,
scene/dialogue-driven escalation, ensemble casting committed at acquisition
(low confidence on "dialogue-forward prose" specifically — inferred from casting and
structure, not sampled chapters).

### Negative space

Traditional epic/secondary-world fantasy absent (fantasy enters only as
romantasy/dark-romance); quiet realist literary fiction absent — literary voice is
bought only attached to a genre engine or transgressive hook. **LitRPG is NOT negative
space**: Seth Ring's Exlian Syndrome (book 6, 2026) shows they buy progression-flavored
series where audio economics work. Cozy mystery, extreme horror, hard-SF idea fiction:
thin to absent in the sample.

## 3. Cross-house invariants (the market taste vector)

Where two very different houses converge, the signal is about the market, not the house:

1. **Premise hook is the first selector.** Both acquire one-sentence-pitchable,
   single-twist premises: Aethon's high-concept lens on a familiar chassis, Blackstone's
   contained trap. Nobody acquires on prose first.
2. **A legible emotional contract, kept.** Aethon: effort→reward fairness. Blackstone:
   secrets surface, the twist lands. The blurb is a promise structure and the book is
   judged on payoff — the same promise/payoff shape LitHarness instruments at §71.
3. **Voice is the named craft selector** at both houses ("strong voice"; "a voice and a
   story") — not sentence ornament.
4. **Transparent prose is the register.** Prose-forward writing is negative space at
   both. The market's selection function runs on story-delivery dimensions.
5. **Series economics** shape everything downstream (open-ended at Aethon, franchise-or-
   sequelized at Blackstone) — irrelevant to scene-grain judging, recorded for later.

## 4. Implications for LitHarness

### 4a. The strategic read on the pairwise bar

The genre's published prose is *tolerated* on exactly the dimensions the first human
read (2026-08-18) named as defects in system prose: Goodreads criticism of Aethon
flagships names "too much telling," stat-sheet bloat, thin interiority — and those books
win reader preference anyway. Two consequences:

- **Matching the genre contract is the ante**: progression clarity, competence-porn
  beats, voice (snark or stoic-rational), effort→reward fairness. A scene that fails
  these loses to published prose regardless of sentence quality.
- **The published comparator is beatable where its own readers already complain**:
  interiority, telling-vs-showing, stat-block integration. Exceeding there while
  holding the ante is the plausible route to a >0.5 lower bound — "superhuman" in this
  frame means *keeping the genre's promises with fewer of the genre's tolerated
  defects*, not importing literary register the frame's readers don't select for.

### 4b. Premise-side reference (plan-level search, Add 3)

The K-alternatives premise search could score candidates against the market taste
vector: single high-concept twist on a familiar chassis; rational-underdog MC with an
up-only curve; found-family accretion slot; humor register declared. The current
frontier premises sit off-lane: **The Toll Gate** is a somber System-debt premise —
drudgery as the world rather than the thing escaped, no opportunity-framing, no
companion slot; **The Vane House** is, amusingly, Blackstone-shaped almost exactly
(contained space + concealed past + reveal structure) rather than Aethon-shaped.
Off-lane premises are fine for harness probes but hostile ground for the headline
pairwise claim, because genre readers' preference is trained on the lanes above.

### 4c. Comparator covariates (preference-runbook §1)

Premise-archetype **lane** should be a matching covariate alongside source/genre/era/
words: cultivation-twist prose vs cozy-crafting prose vs system-apocalypse prose differ
in register, and 2025–26 lanes differ from the 2021 cohort. Era matching matters more
than it looked before this sweep.

### 4d. Candidate annotation dimensions (from stated + revealed taste)

- **Unpredictability** (Aethon's stated criterion; no current instrument).
- **Emotional-contract delivery** — does the scene pay an effort→reward beat the text
  set up (adjacent to §71's promise/payoff, narrower and genre-specific).
- **Voice presence** — snark/stoic register held consistently (reader-named defect
  territory; no dedicated instrument).
- **Interiority without drag** — the differentiator dimension from §4a.

### 4e. Length norms

Aethon: doorstoppers, short chapters, open series. Blackstone: tight 90–120k. Neither
bears on scene-grain judging; both matter if a whole-book claim is ever staged
(craft-corpus covariates already demand length matching — this records the targets).
