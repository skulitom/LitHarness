# Read checklist
Never model-facing: no line of it reaches a prompt, a brief or any model. The coordinator hand-reads the pitch
and every chapter against it, line by line, never delegated, and records residuals only, never a pass.
IDs are from legacy tools/chapter_one_items.json; PARTIAL is a residual; [check] = code counts part, not all.
## Pitch: bible and listing (were the concept, listing and world gates)
- C1 The exception belongs to one person and works for him in chapter 1.
- C3 A physical threat, and where it first reaches him.
- C4 No debt, ledger, court or tenancy frame in the premise, the threat or the System's look.
- C-money No household money or obligation in his before, his want or the world, even in passing. [money]
- L1 Genre words: the System as the book names it, his power working and a faster climb; no rules explained.
- L2 The exception it sells is one person's.
- L3 No institution-issued engine and no money or obligation frame, even repaying or gain taken from others.
- L4 No internal schema word in the title or listing. [leak]
- L5 His situation, not a list of facts: the first sentence carries the change, power or danger, each sentence
  follows the last, and it ends on what he will try or what could go wrong. Would a reader stop at it?
- L6 No word a shelf reader stops on (technical, trade or invented) unless the page gives its meaning.
- W1 No administrative pressure in bible, state or plan. [money at pitch; admin report after]
- W-money Nothing the draft sees (brief, bible, state, plan) holds money or obligation, even negated. [money]
- Retired: C2, no `system.start_rank` (pitch-shape counts Ladder); W2, code sets Start; W3, no world graph.
## Chapter ("ch 1" marks chapter-1 items)
- H1 The exception is his alone and works on the page.
- H2 No money, owing or admin register, even in a joke or an errand; genre costs are exempt. [money, admin]
- H3 System numbers present, the sheet renders, and he opens his status as scene business. [rise, fields]
- H4 Progression felt: the Rise is earned by an act on the page, and lands early in ch 1. [rise]
- H5 A man in his twenties, age on the page, with a before the reader has lived; relatable first. [literals]
- H6 Attention on the pursuit, not one procedure.
- H7 Tone matches the popcorn shelf: light and quick, never grey or dense.
- H8 Third person throughout. [person]
- H-open (ch 1) Paragraph one is in the change, the danger or a moving want; a chore or errand first fails.
- H-diction No trade, technical or invented noun unexplained, nor one he would not use; System terms exempt.
