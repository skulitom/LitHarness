# measurement: Research/measurement surface: what survived, which rules to keep, the minimal eval harness, and what to do with research/

The research surface is the single largest source of drag, and very little of it limits what the rebuild must do.

**What it costs.** research/ has 1,130 tracked files, 1.23M lines and 50 MB. Of that, 259 .py files hold 100,615 lines, and 105 of those files import litharness internals. Research code is loaded by about 160 test files (about 41.5k lines), which hold about 2,380 of the 4,592 test functions. The slowest CI tests are mostly research instruments or prose-citation checks, taking 26–76 s each. 512 of 1,194 commits touch research/. Since 2026-09-08 that is 128 of 183 commits, against 62 that touch src/. The decision ledger alone is 2.0 MB, has 266 entries plus 520 sub-entries, and 346 commits touched it.

**What survived.** Almost all of it is negative knowledge that limits evaluation design:
- Pairwise LLM verdicts are dominated by presentation position.
- LLM readers that do not have to give anything up return the same answer for every text.
- Same-family panels prefer our generated surface text and cannot tell when story order is destroyed.
- A single generated draft varies as much as the effect being tested, so one draft per arm cannot show an effect.
- A one-call count of located features has a reliability of 0.54.
- 22 static text statistics are dead as quality measures.

The one positive reader result is a costed allocation reader: it reads a shuffled book less, and this replicated. It costs about $50 and 2 hours per 180-session arm and needs a shelf of 20 books. It detects only loud damage and meets about one of the ten qualification fields. It belongs to research, not to a development loop.

**Recommendation.** Tag the final state and remove research/, the ledger, RESEARCH.md, the claim-state machinery and the inert editorial control plane from the working tree. Do not split research into a separate repo now: its code cannot run without the old package. Carry forward:
- a 1–2 page LEARNINGS.md;
- an improved version of the Lite benchmark (about 200–300 lines, on demand, never in CI). It should run every pair in both orders, including the damage control; use two drafts from the same system as the null instead of the primed identical-text case; use 4 or more drafts per arm and 3 or more briefs before claiming an advantage; keep the verbatim-quote check; and add code checks for named regressions.

**Risks.** The gitignored local data (derived/ 343 MB, corpora 24 MB, runs/) and the current uncommitted working tree could be lost during the wipe. The repo is public, so corpus-leak protection is still needed.

## Learnings
- [high] Pairwise LLM verdicts are dominated by presentation position; one order is uninterpretable and one swapped pair is weak evidence.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. stage-0 §89 (plan/stage-0-decisions.md:6189-6198): gemma-3-4b-it verdict 'A' on 106/106 passes, answer distribution |positional| 0.9998 vs |text| 0.000214; Haiku 'which would you keep reading' chose-A 0.6408 on 142 decided -> VOID. BRIEF.md:74-77 RevisionBench 43-65% positional artifacts. research/opening-parity/FINDINGS.md:82-88: Haiku persona panel first-slot share 0.85 against its own ordered source (took whichever shown first 17/20). Costed reader slot shares 0.622/0.190/0.105/0.082 (§222). No position-bias measurement exists for gpt-6-astra, the Lite judge (grep of research/ found none).
  IMPLICATION: Every judged pair (including the damage and null controls) runs in both orders. An order-inconsistent preference counts as a tie, and first-slot share is reported. Lite's benchmark.py:126 runs the damage control only with the damaged copy in slot A, so a judge that leans to slot B passes it for free. Fix that before relying on the result.
- [high] Same-family LLM readers reward machine surface text and, at opening length, cannot see story order. An LLM judge is a relative diagnostic between two machine outputs, never a parity or quality certificate.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. §195.5 (stage-0:21833-21840) and opening-parity FINDINGS:77-81: a paragraph-shuffled opening of ours was preferred over The Primal Hunter 20/20 and over Defiance 18/20. §140/§141: the panel preferred our listings over the market's best 15 of 16 pairs and 24/24 over the operator's favourites (RESEARCH.md:163; stage-0:13985, 14347). §89 notes: five judges preferred a told-not-shown repair at 0.92-1.00 (memory latent-taste-track-state). §199.1 (stage-0:22248-22256): the production readers lanes continued 4/4 on shuffled copies. Lite's single damage read did detect even/odd paragraph reordering (REPORT.md:129), which is necessary but not sufficient.
  IMPLICATION: Treat a same-family judge as a diagnostic that locates defects and requires quotes, not as the acceptance decision. An order-damage control is mandatory in every run. Prefer a cross-family judge (Claude reading Codex output, or the reverse) when available. Never compare against human or market text with a same-family judge and read the result as parity.
- [high] A cooperative reader that can answer at no cost saturates. Absolute 'would you continue' or rating instruments carry no variance and cannot rank sibling drafts.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. BRIEF.md:199-201: persona gate-0 returned 195/196 keep-reading, with ms_between = ms_within = 0. BRIEF.md:131 and §227: anticipation probe arm means span 0.0080 against a 0.05 floor, and the whitespace placebo moved it more than destaking did (800 calls, $30.65). §134 (stage-0:13517-13526): 6 of 8 drafts scored 4/4 on continuation; Mann-Whitney p = 1.000.
  IMPLICATION: Use no absolute LLM scores as gates, no best-of-n selection by an LLM continuation verdict and no persona panels. If a reader instrument is ever rebuilt, its reader must give something up to continue.
- [high] The draft is the unit of variance, so a single generation per arm is an anecdote. One-call counts of located features are unreliable.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. §133/§134: one prompt drafted repeatedly spans the effect range, and four drafts a side still gave p = 1.000. comic-beats-results.md:18,261-268: a one-call chapter locator has reliability 0.54 and a correlation ceiling of 0.73; four draws per unit are needed for 0.8 (memory comic-beat-census). Lite REPORT.md:84-85 itself notes that its two reads are of one pair.
  IMPLICATION: The harness must support k>=4 drafts per arm and >=3 briefs before claiming a general advantage, which is also Lite REPORT.md next step 3. Located-count diagnostics use >=4 draws or report population statistics only.
- [high] The only reader mechanism that survived controls is a costed allocation reader. It detects only whole-book-scale order damage, at research-grade cost and substrate.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. cost-that-bites/FINDINGS-v3.md:24-32: intact minus shuffled +0.1640 [+0.0881, +0.2390], replicated at +0.1890 [+0.0747, +0.2955]; 180/180 scorable; $49.78 and $50.35; 2h04 each. §259: 65% partial shuffle +0.194 [+0.134, +0.252], but on a reader that had drifted by +0.153. §222: slot-A lean consumed 3/4 of v1. It needs a 20-book fitness shelf, and a book needs about 3,900 words of chapters (BRIEF.md:242-250). It meets arguably 1 of 10 QualificationEvidence fields (BRIEF.md:235-237; src/litharness/domain/editorial.py:63).
  IMPLICATION: Do not port fcr/cost_that_bites, bcr, or the editorial control plane that waits on a qualified mechanism (application/editorial.py 930 lines plus domain/editorial.py 403 lines, inert). Record the design idea in LEARNINGS.md and leave the code at the tag.
- [high] Static text statistics are dead as quality measures, but deterministic counters remain valid as named-defect detectors and regression checks.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. BRIEF.md §2 heading: 22 proxies dead. For example, tricolon_rate separated declared-AI text at 0.629 against pre-2023 prose, but undeclared 2025 text separated at 0.606, so it detects the year (BRIEF.md:57-62). CDG's rename sham moved 2.0x the strongest damage (BRIEF.md:111). Survivors: scene_echo/repeated_span, which claim repetition only (BRIEF.md:94-98); a tells counter finding 5 families at 3-10x the shelf rate (§199; src/litharness/domain/tells.py, 362 lines); and our prose is separable from human prose at AUC 1.0 by 24 surface counts (§75, RESEARCH.md:418).
  IMPLICATION: The eval harness gets cheap code checks tied to concrete defects: brief-literal facts on the page, named resource accounting (Lite's age and breath defects), repeated spans and tell rates. They report numbers, are never a quality score and are never a selection target (the Goodhart rule, BRIEF.md:349-351).
- [high] Model-call isolation is a measured validity threat. A CLI call made from inside the repo can see git status and the path.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. §258 (stage-0:26712-26730): claude -p 2.1.280 returned an untracked filename in 2 of 5 calls and the working directory in about 2 of 5, despite --system-prompt. Every Claude reader arm from 2026-08-17 to 09-22 was exposed. Lite runs generation and judge calls from empty directories with tools and config off (PROTOCOL.md:20-21).
  IMPLICATION: Keep Lite's empty-temp-directory, tools-off transport. Keep one isolation test to rerun after a CLI upgrade.
- [high] The governance ceremony consumed most recent effort and could not keep up with itself.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. 512/1194 commits touch research/, and since 2026-09-08 128/183, against 62 touching src/ (git rev-list). The ledger is 2,001,354 bytes and 27,439 lines, with 346 commits touching it. There are 73 claim.json files and 116 PREREG/registration/AMENDMENT/FAILURE/RECOVERY files. About 28 production commits of 09-08..09-12 went unledgered (RESEARCH.md:480-483). The promise-payoff line 'closed unrun' after two login failures and a wrapper defect under the retry rules (research/quality-measurement/README.md:30). The prose-symbol resolver test_every_symbol_the_prose_names_still_exists is the slowest CI test at 44-62 s. OPINION: the extra structure bought little beyond what the few rules below provide.
  IMPLICATION: Replace claim states, the ledger, RESEARCH.md, research_overview.py and its test (546 lines), and epistemic_governance.py and its test (412 lines) with one LEARNINGS.md, plus one folder per experiment holding a frozen protocol, input hashes, raw receipts and a report, as Lite does.
- [medium] Declared bars and controls often cannot do what they claim. Dry runs and simulated nulls caught most failures before spend.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED. Declared quantities that could not do what they claimed: §81, §85 and §87, plus seven more in the §89 table (stage-0:6214-6230, five of them caught before spend), and an eighth at §147 (a 5.88 display-rounded ceiling). The compression order-asymmetry control was algebraically antisymmetric and 'passes on pure noise' (BRIEF.md:91). The slope estimator's bias at n=32 is 0.436 against 0.482 (BRIEF.md:100-104).
  IMPLICATION: Keep one rule: before paying for a comparison, dry-run it and check that each control can fail and that the unit counted is independent. Lite's identical-text null is primed by its prompt ('If the texts are identical, say they are indistinguishable', benchmark.py:24-25), so that control can hardly fail. Replace it with a comparison of two drafts from the same system.
- [medium] The production improvements the record credits came from changing what a call is shown, not from reader instruments.
  EVIDENCE: MEASURED and documented. RESEARCH.md:472-477: 'the defect was in what a call was shown, not in the model'. RESEARCH.md:246 (§135-§138): two words of brief outweighed every rule, and a rule's affirmative half is what gets obeyed. §262: the operator's rejected money register came back through our own prompt text. §110/§111: showing the ledger or world raised uptake from 0 to 8/40 and from 0/12 to 17/17. None of the reader instruments ever steered production.
  IMPLICATION: Invest in small, inspectable prompts and context, and in located operator reads as regression cases, not in measurement machinery. Rerun the tag's replay or diagnostic tools only when a concrete question needs them.

## Risks
- Gitignored local research data is destroyed by a careless wipe (for example git clean -fdx). This includes research/quality-measurement/derived (343 MB), corpora (24 MB, including toll.db and the fitness shelf) and runs/. None of it is in git history. => Before the wipe, copy these directories outside the repo (for example C:/DEV/LitHarness-archive-data), or leave them untouched and remove only tracked paths with git rm.
- The current dirty working tree is lost or excluded from the archive tag: 13 modified src/test/doc files plus untracked tools/produce.py, scene_inspection.py, test_produce.py and research raw volume-screen files. => Commit it to a branch, or deliberately discard it with the operator's say, before creating the archive tag. Tag the commit that holds it and push the tag. Memory says never force-push.
- Negative knowledge is lost and the rebuild re-buys dead instruments: absolute LLM scores, persona panels, best-of-n by LLM verdict, static text statistics. => Put LEARNINGS.md at the top of the new repo with the dead-instrument table and the tag name, and make the harness enforce both orders, a sibling null and k draws.
- Lite's promising n=1 result is over-read. The judge is the same model family (gpt-6-astra) as the generator, its position bias was never measured, the damage control ran in one orientation, and the identity null is primed by the prompt. => Run the fixed harness on at least 3 briefs with at least 4 drafts per arm before claiming an advantage. Measure the judge's first-slot share on sibling pairs. Add a cross-family judge where available.
- The repo is public (gh repo view: PUBLIC). After the wipe, benchmark baselines or market comparison text could put third-party prose into history. => Keep corpora outside the repo. Replace the full-history audit with a cheap check on new blobs only, limiting the length of any quoted non-generated text.
- Resuming reader research later is harder, because the research code depends on the deleted package. => If reader research is funded again, clone the archive tag into a separate (preferably private) research repo or worktree. Do not vendor the old package into the new tree.
