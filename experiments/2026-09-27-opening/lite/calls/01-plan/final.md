# One Slot, Open Water

## Chapter frame
Approximately **3,962 words**, close third person, past tense, entirely through the protagonist. The opening should deliver one complete rescue attempt while exposing a much larger disaster.

## Protagonist and present desire
**Elias Venn, 26**, a council drainage worker on probation after falsifying a safety inspection. Practical, ashamed, and accustomed to making temporary fixes look permanent.

When the System arrived, Elias was inspecting a road underpass. Now a bus was stranded there, water was rising, and his seventeen-year-old brother, **Jamie**, was trapped beneath a collapsed seat frame.

**Immediate desire:** get Jamie onto dry ground before the bus flooded. Elias did not initially want power, answers, or responsibility for the other passengers.

## Supporting characters
- **Jamie Venn:** wanted Elias to rescue an injured passenger first. He knew about the falsified inspection and distrusted his brother’s assurances.
- **Saira Holt, bus driver:** wanted the greatest number of passengers out alive, even if abandoning the trapped became necessary. She needed Elias’s knowledge but would not surrender command.
- **Mara Bell, senior drainage engineer:** wanted to open the emergency sluice before floodwater reached an occupied underground station. Doing so would send a violent surge through the underpass. She was neither mistaken nor indifferent.

## Minimum world rules
- Every person on Earth received **exactly one Slot for one skill**. Elias also received exactly one Slot, but his could hold **as many skills as he could take**. His exception concerned capacity, not additional Slots.
- The System’s arrival imposed new structures on existing geography. A vast obstruction now crossed the river; displaced water was overwhelming nearby drainage.
- Small, solid **skill kernels** appeared among the new structures. Touching one revealed its function; deliberate acceptance consumed it. Ordinary occupied Slots could not accept another skill.
- Skills produced narrow, reliable effects. Using them demanded physical effort and attention; possession did not supply judgment or protect a user from drowning.
- Establish only three skills this chapter: **Cling**, **Wedge**, and **Breath Vault**. Leave acquisition beyond these initial kernels unexplained.

## Four connected dramatic movements

### 1. The shortest route — roughly 800 words
**Cause:** The river obstruction appeared, communications became unreliable, and floodwater stalled the bus.

**Choice:** Elias abandoned his assigned inspection to reach Jamie. He concealed how quickly the underpass could fill, fearing Saira would order everyone to leave Jamie behind.

**Consequence:** Saira committed passengers to a rescue position she would not otherwise have chosen. Elias reached the bus, but his lie made him responsible for people now relying on unsafe information. His Slot displayed an unsettling capacity symbol he had no time to investigate.

### 2. A second acceptance — roughly 1,000 words
**Cause:** New stonework protruding beside the bus held several kernels. Saira took Cling and used it to establish an evacuation line. Elias took Wedge to brace the collapsing seat frame.

**Choice:** When the water reached Jamie’s chin, Elias tried accepting Breath Vault despite already having a skill.

**Consequence:** It worked. He could carry a small reserve of breathable air underwater, but could not simultaneously maintain the brace and move Jamie without help. Saira witnessed his second skill. His advantage became a practical dependency, not an immediate escape.

### 3. Tell them what the water will do — roughly 1,150 words
**Cause:** Mara reached them by maintenance radio: she needed the sluice opened locally to protect the station. Elias alone knew the manual release route.

**Choice:** Elias admitted the true flood timing and offered a sequence: evacuate the mobile passengers, free Jamie, then open the sluice. Saira rejected his optimistic margin. Jamie insisted they include the injured passenger.

**Consequence:** They adopted a harsher plan requiring Elias to stay behind and operate the release underwater. He surrendered control of Jamie’s rescue to Saira.

### 4. The useful thing he cannot undo — roughly 1,012 words
**Cause:** Debris jammed the sluice before it could fully open.

**Choice:** Elias used Wedge to force it permanently open rather than preserve a route back.

**Consequence:** The pressure eased toward the station; the current swept Elias through the maintenance channel. His last glimpse confirmed Jamie had reached Saira’s line—not dry ground.

## Unresolved ending
Elias surfaced inside an unfamiliar chamber beneath the new river obstruction. His air reserve was empty. Across the only apparent exit, something living wore skill kernels embedded in its skin.

Above him, Jamie still needed rescuing. Elias had just destroyed the way back.
